# Codex dot coordination

Use when the user addresses dot directly or assigns it the coordinating role. Dot retains the goal and returns the result. For a [small bounded result](../SKILL.md#choose-the-owner), commission an internal worker and use a separate permitted non-author when independent checking is required. Dot [reviews its outgoing work orders](../SKILL.md#review-each-outgoing-assignment) to either type of recipient. An ordinary agent's position in a tree supplies no dot mandate.

## Choose the category for this assignment

| Category | Arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| В | One coordinator assigns the subject result to a worker and each required independent check to a non-author. The workers can be internal for bounded assignments. No standing reviewer or secretary is required. |
| Б | Executor writes and corrects the subject result. Reviewer independently checks it, returns full findings to dot and owns accompanying content and navigation. |
| А | Category Б plus a secretary for the assigned protocol, navigation and authorized record responsibilities. Reviewer retains accompanying content. |

Use В when one lead can manage the work. Consider Б when role separation and accompaniment ownership are useful, and А when the work justifies separate protocol and navigation responsibilities. Select the role arrangement independently of the model, tree depth and number of user sessions.

A multi-pass Issue in this dot protocol requires Б or А even if each pass is small. Verify the category and assigned roles before dispatch or dependent continuation. If the saved arrangement is В, establish Б/А within existing authority or identify the missing assignment. Preserve the current pass, cycle, verified work, open findings and grants. A category correction does not restart dispatch or create a session.

## Assign areas before editing

Identify the agreed stage result, its scope and completion criteria. Assign a writer and a non-author checker for each changed area under the dot protocol. Name the responsible participants and evidence returns before edits.

| Area | Writer in Б | Writer in А | Independent check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject result, including requested manuals, skill contracts, tests or settings | Executor | Executor | Subject reviewer |
| Accompaniment, such as usage explanation, durable limitations or supporting knowledge | Reviewer | Reviewer | Executor read-only or another non-author |
| Navigation, such as maps, indexes and reading routes | Reviewer | Secretary | Assigned non-author, which may be the reviewer for secretary changes when uninvolved |
| Assigned protocol or authorization records | Named record owner | Named record owner, with the secretary responsible for the assigned acceptance record | Non-author checks a record relied on for consolidation against the actual result, review, grant and retained context |

In В the lead explicitly assigns needed areas and checks. Requested documentation is subject work. The executor in Б/А may read accompaniment and supply facts or findings, while its assigned writer makes any changes. Choose one writer for a mixed-purpose file before edits and route other participants' contributions to that writer. Record the scope, fixed revision and new writer for a necessary serialized transfer. Prior authorship remains relevant to independence.

Before changing navigation or moving project knowledge, establish the authoritative sources, affected routes, agreed inventory and mapping, write scope and non-author checks. Preserve source identity, provenance, uncertain material and valid adaptations. A secretary's assigned role alone supplies no authority to delete, download, republish or change substantive conclusions.

## Keep roles and evidence through corrections

Within a stage, dot remains arbiter, each area's writer makes its corrections, and its checker remains read-only for that area. A reviewer may run checks or make isolated diagnostic examples where permitted. A separately assigned area can have that reviewer as writer and another participant as checker. Deliver complete findings to dot without executor filtering.

Fix the review target and confirm access to the agreed baseline, exact result and evidence. Use an accessible local revision when it meets the task's delivery requirements.

A correction, replacement, new revision or status change is still within the same stage. An unavailable executor may be replaced by a new executor after its writes are settled as described in [interrupted assignment](../SKILL.md#recover-an-interrupted-assignment). Keep subject corrections with an executor, including a quick fix to the reviewer's own finding. Exchange roles only after the present stage is complete and another result stage actually begins. Announce the assignments before changes. Retain the history and consider earlier authorship when selecting checks. Carry existing grants across internal stages within their scope and respect any reserved major-pass decision.

After every two correction cycles without readiness, reassess progress, repeated causes, verification strategy and remaining work. Reassess earlier on recurring regressions, non-progress, contradictory requirements or missing critical evidence. Change the approach when its failure is demonstrated, and record the next observable checkpoint or stopping condition. A cycle count alone does not change category, create a user decision, weaken the criteria or reset history. Continue concrete in-scope corrections while available, using only actual limits.

## Decide a category or session change

Keep the arrangement while it yields credible checks and progress. Consider a change when an evidenced quality problem survives targeted repairs, regressions recur, checks repeatedly miss demonstrable issues, a consequential disagreement survives a bounded factual check, or needed independence or access is demonstrably absent. Diagnose the cause so the proposed change addresses it. More sessions cannot supply missing requirements or authorization.

For an in-scope category change, record the cause, scope, expected benefit and cost, owners and next checkpoint. Preserve the required minimum category and the roles for unfinished areas. For a material tradeoff or new permission, return the decision to its owner.

Before adding a separate user session, establish its actual launch, access and return capabilities. State the failed criterion, observed evidence, attempted remedies, benefit, cost, scope and stopping condition. Use an existing explicit grant only if it covers this transition. Otherwise obtain the user's agreement before launching. Continue separable work while that decision or capability is unavailable and identify any required check still missing.

## Continue and return the conclusion

On a fresh or transferred session, apply the [interrupted assignment](../SKILL.md#recover-an-interrupted-assignment) contract. Recover the active unit/pass, cycle, stage boundary, category, area and record owners, and granted session scope as part of the live state. Keep the required method instructions with their exact pins through review and correction. Record actual checking separately from the assignments.

Return the fixed result and full evidence to the responsible participant, with a [correction decision](adjudicate.md), [readiness conclusion](assess-readiness.md) or a named reserved decision. When asked about an acceptance record, establish the actual reviewed result, the scope of the user's authorization and the record's observed state. In А the assigned secretary is the record owner. In В/Б identify one responsible record owner if needed. Give the recorder the verified evidence and authorization for the specified decision. Arbitration concludes with that handoff.
