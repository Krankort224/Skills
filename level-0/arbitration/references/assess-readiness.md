# Assess readiness

Use for a proposed finished result or a stage gate. Judge the full agreed result at an immutable combined revision or equivalently fixed artifact set that the required checkers can access. Record the baseline and exact target.

## Check the combined result

1. Map each requirement to its result and actual evidence. Inspect the combined changes for preserved behavior, cross-area consistency, unfulfilled commitments and out-of-scope effects. Identify which versions were actually checked and who authored and checked each relevant area.
2. Reconcile the complete findings without writer filtering. A material unresolved defect or missing mandatory check keeps its gate open. No participant independently approves their own work, including work by a coordinator, reviewer or record owner. If independent checking is required and unavailable, state the precise gap and assess the remaining evidence without substituting self-review.
3. Account for each writer that can affect this result, including pending, queued and potentially resumable work. Establish settlement and collect or verify the resulting revision. Working safely on disjoint areas does not settle an uncertain writer. If further writes cannot be excluded, identify the affected area and open gate. Use the [interrupted assignment](../SKILL.md#recover-an-interrupted-assignment) contract to resolve it.
4. Recheck changed areas and verification invalidated by changed inputs or dependencies, including cross-area consistency. Carry forward unaffected evidence with grounds for its continued applicability. An old review of a different combined revision is evidence only for the properties that survived.

## Verify affected documentation

Identify material documentation impacts when assigning work and revisit them at integration and after corrections. Use authoritative sources to locate each applicable contract, design, configuration, operating instruction, usage note, knowledge reference or navigation route and its actual owner and authorized destination. Required documentation can be affected even if omitted from planning or stored elsewhere. Leave unrelated or optional documentation debt outside the gate.

Assign required updates within the current boundary and verify them against primary evidence and the final combined result. Include independent checking of mandatory changed areas and affected routes and dependencies. When no update is required, retain a source-based conclusion where that matters to the decision. When evidence, access, write permission or independent verification is missing, identify the document, owner, gap, consequence and smallest authorized remedy. Continue separable work, keeping that named gate open.

## Identify authority and verify records

A readiness conclusion is evidence for the decision owner. Acceptance, publication, merge and closure each require their own authority. An explicit prior grant remains usable for its stated result or intermediate unit after a role or interface change. Verify the grant against live state and the actual reviewed result. Identify the authorized record owner and give them the checked result and scope, without asking again solely because the interface or owner changed. A grant for intermediate acceptance does not cover final acceptance or closure by implication.

Before relying on an acceptance record to replace source material, have a non-author compare it with the reviewed revision, findings, actual authorization and durable information needed to continue. Verify the real record and references by readback when published. Establish record publication, removal authority and cleanup completion from their respective evidence. If the record, access or removal capability is missing, report the pending action and affected sources by identifier so they remain available and the owner can act within their authority. End the assessment by returning the verified evidence or gap to that owner.

## Ready or blocked

Return the exact result and which checks support it, the material findings and dispositions, documentation and writer state, and any limitation affecting use. If an agreed gate remains open, name the missing evidence or decision, its effect and the next responsible participant or remedy. Report optional improvements separately without adding new readiness requirements. End at the supported readiness or correction conclusion or the reserved decision.
