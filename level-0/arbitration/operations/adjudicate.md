# Adjudicate findings

Use when review findings arrive or material disagreement affects the agreed result. Preserve complete original findings and each disposition on the existing task surface. Give the responsible decision-maker the full review without writer filtering. Keep the review's observations, subsequent clarifications and the arbiter's disposition attributable to their sources.

## Establish the claim

Record a stable finding ID, the agreed requirement and authoritative source, exact reviewed revision, observed deviation and evidence, consequence, disposition with grounds, and observable closure condition. Separate facts from inferences and an unperformed check from a failed check. Check evidence at its owner. Confidence, severity and an author's assurance cannot establish a claim on their own.

## Choose a disposition

| Disposition | Basis and action |
| --- | --- |
| Correct | The evidence supports a contract violation, regression or missing required result. Specify the bounded change, preserved behavior and closing evidence. |
| Investigate | A consequential claim has insufficient or conflicting evidence. Specify a discriminating check, accessible inputs, the evidence it would distinguish and its stopping point. Keep the claim open while the uncertainty matters. |
| Reject with grounds | Authoritative evidence contradicts the claim, or the requested change has no applicable requirement behind it. Record the evidence and reasoning. |
| Propose separately | The change could be useful outside the agreed result. Preserve it as a proposal without expanding the current task or treating it as a blocking defect. |
| Escalate | The remaining choice requires reserved authority, a material tradeoff, or unavailable permission or capability after checking remedies. Return the decision to its owner with evidence, options, consequences and a recommendation. Continue separable work. |

A writer and checker may clarify or challenge facts after the initial independent assessment. Choose the smallest check that can resolve the disagreement. For example, reconcile allegedly missing records against the pinned input and output. Treat an unperformed mandatory host check as missing evidence even if the result looks plausible.

## Route correction and verify closure

Combine compatible changes into a practical correction package. For each item give its requirement, target and baseline, allowed write scope, behavior to preserve, assigned writer, independent checker and closure evidence. Keep task-required instructions and stable writer/checker boundaries. Route findings to the area's writer. Do not have a subject reviewer implement their own finding or use an area rename or role switch to permit it. Reassignment must first account for [unfinished writers](../SKILL.md#recover-an-interrupted-assignment).

At the new fixed revision, verify the correction with the area's non-author checker where required and recheck any affected dependencies. Identify still-valid prior evidence by scope. Changed behavior can invalidate documentation, navigation, interfaces or another contribution's check even when its files did not change. An author's correction claim is insufficient evidence of closure. If mandatory documentation needs updating, route it to its authorized writer and require its applicable check at the combined revision.

Return every material finding with its actual disposition, evidence and next action, including an unresolved gap or reserved decision. Use [readiness assessment](assess-readiness.md) for the resulting combined state when needed.
