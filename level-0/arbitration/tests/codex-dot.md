# Codex dot decision criteria

Use the [raw Codex dot cases](inputs/codex-dot.md) and the [common run instructions](common.md#apply-the-cases). The category, area and progress rules here exercise the [dot protocol](../references/codex-dot.md).

| ID | Observable result | Owning route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Uses В for this proportionate simple arrangement. Obtains a non-author check of the fixed result without requiring a standing specialist or another user session. | [Category](../references/codex-dot.md#choose-the-category-for-this-assignment) |
| C2 | Stops dependent work under В and requires Б/А with concrete role and area assignments. Preserves the active pass, cycle, grants, findings and history. Does not redispatch or automatically open another session. | [Category](../references/codex-dot.md#choose-the-category-for-this-assignment) |
| C3 | Routes the subject fix to the executor and recheck to the reviewer at a fixed new revision, even for one line. | [Corrections](../references/codex-dot.md#keep-roles-and-evidence-through-corrections) |
| C4 | Assigns accompaniment to the reviewer. The executor can supply facts and serve as a non-author checker if independent for the note, while staying read-only. | [Areas](../references/codex-dot.md#assign-areas-before-editing) |
| C7 | Checks navigation at its exact revision against source and surviving routes. The uninvolved reviewer or another non-author checks the secretary's changes. | [Areas](../references/codex-dot.md#assign-areas-before-editing) |
| C8 | Establishes need, source inventory, provenance, movement map, authority and non-author checks for justified changes. Protects uncertain sources, does not infer permission to delete or change conclusions from the secretary role, and surfaces reserved decisions. | [Areas](../references/codex-dot.md#assign-areas-before-editing) |
| C9 | Replaces the settled unavailable executor under existing authority with a new writer. Preserves reviewer independence, active stage, findings and correction history. | [Corrections](../references/codex-dot.md#keep-roles-and-evidence-through-corrections) |
| C11 | Verifies the explicit intermediate grant and successful review of the exact result against live state. Identifies the new authorized recorder without repeating the permission request. Reports what is actually recorded or pending and returns checked scope/evidence without treating the decision as final acceptance or closure. | [Dot conclusion](../references/codex-dot.md#continue-and-return-the-conclusion) and [authority](../operations/assess-readiness.md#identify-authority-and-verify-records) |
| C15 | Reassesses repeat cause and evidence after two cycles, changes a demonstrated failed approach and identifies an observable next check or stop condition. Preserves history, criteria and category unless a diagnosed reason warrants change. | [Corrections](../references/codex-dot.md#keep-roles-and-evidence-through-corrections) |
| C16 | A non-author checks actual result, review, grant and surviving continuation information. A draft does not justify removal. Published readback must match checked scope and preserve evidence and necessary references. An unsupported or unavailable action stays pending with exact source IDs and owner. | [Authority and records](../operations/assess-readiness.md#identify-authority-and-verify-records) |
| C19 | Returns the note change to its reviewer-writer and assigns a non-author to check it and affected consistency at r2. The reviewer stays read-only on the subject. Identifies invalidated r1 evidence. | [Corrections](../references/codex-dot.md#keep-roles-and-evidence-through-corrections) and [documentation](../operations/assess-readiness.md#verify-affected-documentation) |
| C20 | Secretary updates authorized navigation, a non-author verifies the new routes, and unrelated historical debt creates no automatic write or gate. | [Areas](../references/codex-dot.md#assign-areas-before-editing) and [documentation](../operations/assess-readiness.md#verify-affected-documentation) |
| C25 | Accepts direct intake to dot and organizes Б with the executor and an authorized internal non-author reviewer in the same user session. Supplies raw artifacts and fixed inputs, keeps the reviewer read-only on the subject and does not create another user session, permanent roster or management loop. | [Category](../references/codex-dot.md#choose-the-category-for-this-assignment) and [corrections](../references/codex-dot.md#keep-roles-and-evidence-through-corrections) |
| C26 | Diagnoses the repeated review failure with evidence and checks proposed session capability, cost, benefit, scope and stopping point. Keeps existing grants and seeks the reserved decision before a separate user session when no grant covers it. Continues allowed disjoint work. | [Session change](../references/codex-dot.md#decide-a-category-or-session-change) |
