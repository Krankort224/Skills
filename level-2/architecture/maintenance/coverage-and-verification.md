## Evaluation and locality

formula. Each selected increment has an ordinary input, fixed conflict and changed
condition. Check the same demand/boundary before and after; preserve negative and
conditional results. Exercise new values in a fresh context as well as inherited

its complete relevant unit. Follow only a named unresolved prerequisite or actual
consequence; reuse material and permit genuine reciprocal influences without
unbounded traversal. Sources are inspected separately for provenance, not secretly
counted as required architectural-task reading. Normative/actual project evidence
is the deliberate exception when its particular claim requires live verification.
