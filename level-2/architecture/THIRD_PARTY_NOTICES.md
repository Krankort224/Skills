## Selected Architecture library literature and course materials

Francis D. K. Ching, *Архитектура: форма, пространство, композиция*, Russian
edition, AST/Astrel, 2005, translated from the 1996 English edition of
*Architecture: Form, Space, & Order*. The inspected copyright leaf retains the
original publisher and Russian translation rights; no open-content license was
established. The supplied composition-course PDF likewise has no verified
redistribution license. Library possession is not a redistribution license.

Only independently written explanations of selected design ideas and an
original hypothetical spatial example are included. No source scans,
illustrations, extensive quotations or full texts are redistributed. Source
identities, exact inspected pages and rejected prescriptions are recorded in
[the library source map](sources/library.md#architecture-library-selected-content).
The two upstream MIT licenses below do not license these books/course materials.

Additional selected readings: Francis D. K. Ching, *Building Construction
Illustrated*, fifth edition, John Wiley & Sons, 2014 (copyright leaf: all rights
reserved); Heino Engel, *Несущие системы*, AST/Astrel, 2007, translated from the
1997 German *Tragsysteme* (Russian translation copyright2006); and Margarita A.
Guryeva, SPbGASU supplied course PDF *Крыши, кровли, покрытия*, date unverified.
No open redistribution license was established for these materials. The same
original-paraphrase/no-source-images boundary above applies; selected pages and
rejected prescriptions are recorded in the library source map.

Additional housing/construction readings: Neufert, Russian 2011 translation of
the 39th revised edition; Guryeva/SPbGASU supplied course PDFs on bearing walls,
floors, balconies and transparent enclosures (dates unverified); and the Turukin
apartment-house and Rogozina quarter coursework PDFs identified in the source
map. No open redistribution license was established. Only original methods,
critical observations and bibliographic/page evidence are included; no source
plan, scan, photograph or course/book text is redistributed. The hypothetical
worked studies are original, not reproductions of those projects.

Additional timber/interface reading: A. B. Shmidt and P. A. Dmitriev,
*Атлас строительных конструкций из клееной древесины и водостойкой фанеры*,
ASV, title/copyright2002 (internal2001 references unresolved), and I. Yu. Anikin's
SPbGASU individual-house coursework2026. No open redistribution license was
established. The installed timber method and terrace study are original
paraphrase/geometry; no atlas scheme, source drawing, scan, photograph or book
text is redistributed. Exact inspected pages and source limits are in
[the timber source map](sources/library.md#timber-joints-and-house-interfaces).
The temporary DjVu decoder and source rasters are not part of this skill.

Additional selected course readings: Gurieva/SPbGASU ASK01 introduction and
construction fundamentals, ASK02 bases/foundations, ASK04 walls/volumetric
blocks/frames and ASK06 lift-stair core. No publication date or open redistribution
license was established. Only original synthesis, source/page evidence and
hypothetical worked geometry are installed; no source slide, photograph,
historical detail, extensive quotation or full course text is redistributed.
Exact inspected file pages and exclusions are in
[the ground/frame/core source map](sources/library.md#ground-frame-and-core-selections).

Additional style/precedent work uses selected pages of the same Ching composition
book and primary building-operator, museum, architect/archive, heritage and
professional-institution sources identified in the individual case analyses and
[source map](sources/index.md). P14's Russian2016 title/copyright/contents
were inspected to identify *European Building Construction Illustrated*, Francis
D. K. Ching and Mark Mulville, Wiley2014/RIPOL2016; it is not used as a style-history
survey. Its copyright leaf reserves reproduction rights.

No open reproduction license for book illustrations, website photographs, plans
or sections is inferred from access, attribution or the software licenses below.
Installed style assets are original authored analyses with precise source links,
not redistributed source media or traced source drawings. Each case states what
was actually inspected and its rights disposition. A later requested image use
requires checking that particular asset's permission and credit. Original
hypothetical transfer fragments are not reproductions or proven performance of
the source buildings.

## Applied site guidance

Selected reading: *Manual for Streets* (Department for Transport/Communities and
Local Government, Thomas Telford Publishing, 2007), *Building Bulletin 103*
(Department for Education, June 2014), and Esri ArcGIS Pro 3.6 location-allocation
documentation. Exact URLs, PDF hashes, inspected sections and rights dispositions
are retained in [applied site sources](sources/applied-site-methods.md).

The Manual's copyright leaf reserves Queen's Printer/Controller of HMSO and
typographic rights with limited reproduction purposes; BB103 states Crown
copyright 2014 and OGL v2.0 excluding logos and separately identified third-party
material. No open redistribution permission for Esri media/software is established.
Only original explanatory synthesis, original geometry/examples and bibliographic
links are installed. No source drawing, diagram, screenshot, table of dimensions,
full text, map or software is redistributed. Access does not expand those rights.

## Solar occlusion sources

Selected primary reading: *Physically Based Rendering: From Theory to
Implementation*, Matt Pharr, Wenzel Jakob and Greg Humphreys, third-edition
online text, section3.1. Its footer identifies copyright2004–2021 and CC
BY-NC-ND4.0 for book text. NOAA's *General Solar Position Calculations* supplies
technical time/solar provenance, not statutory criteria. Exact sources/read
extents and limitations are retained in [solar occlusion](sources/solar-occlusion.md).
Only original mathematics, prose, examples and verification code are installed;
no renderer code, adapted book text, images, diagrams or PDF is redistributed.
No general permission for source media is inferred, and existing MIT notices
remain unchanged.
