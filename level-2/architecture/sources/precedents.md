## Role and local use

Each retained resource below has an explicit publisher/hosting family, role and reason to retain it. Original case/profile text owns the exact inspected paragraph/view and the observed/reported/inferred distinction; the retained adoption map below adds source-specific reading/rejection limits. These are audit locators, not instructions to reload every page. Original visual locators remain necessary for auditing the named view or a later image-use/permission claim; no viewing or attribution grants redistribution. Object and historical interpretation locators retain the factual/interpretive basis, not current engineering/normative authority. The necessary exception is a new factual/image/permission claim: inspect its actual primary evidence rather than infer it from a local adaptation.

Library identifiers retain their shared identity in the [library reading owner](library.md#architecture-library-selected-content). Style maintenance combines selected permitted library fragments with primary
object/architect/museum/heritage evidence. Historical designation, observed form,
interpretation and a new brief's assumed conditions are different claims. The
[selector](../references/composition/styles/index.md) declares a bounded direction/object perimeter;
[general transfer methods](../references/composition/styles/precedent-transfer.md) do not turn that into
an exhaustive history or a performance/compliance claim. Individual authored
cases under `assets/styles/<style>/<building>/` retain their precise source
locators, inspected text/views, missing media and rights disposition. Working
retrieval records and source-page renders remain outside Git.
