Adapted principle: count occupied external fronts and real street/core journeys,
not just facade lines; test their consequences at a corner/end and inside rooms.
Existing [housing source selections](library.md#architecture-library-selected-content)
retain their original Neufert/coursework identities and reading limits; this work
claims no new scan/page inspection. The explicit transforms, original unit,

London plan or a method asserted by that source.

## Verification boundary

Local checks establish specified rectangle/door/front relationships, land/room/
shaft accounting, graph continuity/isolation and declared pressure arithmetic.
They do not establish actual household ergonomics for every use, solar/daylight
performance, flow/losses, equipment selection, rated construction, combustion
safety or regulatory approval. Select [requirements](../references/requirements.md)
and the actual project/system evidence for those claims. Source access, a draft
guideline or a passing graph check does not expand that authority.
