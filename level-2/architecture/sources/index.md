## Locality and necessary external evidence

Use the complete selected local reasoning unit, its conditions, alternatives, quantitative definitions, failure/correction and relevant case. Source IDs resolve provenance; they are not short replacements for methods. If a needed mechanism is missing, preserve the dependent uncertainty and add supported local explanation within the task boundary rather than pretend a bibliography supplies the method.

Live requirements, actual site/survey/operating information, changeable product/engineering evidence and a new original-media/permission claim can require external retrieval. Distinguish that actual claim from applying a locally explained geometric, spatial or organizational method. Source-only/unread records do not become knowledge coverage through relocation.

The [jurisdiction index](../references/normative/index.md) exposes implemented modules only.

secondary-only statements are narrowed rather than relabeled as primary checks.
