# Review

## Inputs

Use the design material, any requested artifacts, brief and accepted baseline, requested stage, requirement sources and calculation/execution evidence. Assess the actual result rather than only its creator's description; a textual concept needs no image unless the task requested one.

## Procedure

1. Establish the review boundary and evidence available. Do not redefine the brief to fit the delivered design.
2. Select the actual program, composition or environmental knowledge section and the critique in [design development](../references/methods/design-development.md). Reconstruct the proposal's room/route/level logic and operating modes from its actual evidence. Examine packing, crossings, floor overlaps/section allowances, facade-to-use relationships and assembly/maintenance consequences. For each closed zone trace its complete boundary and every connection to spaces remaining open, including shared toilets and exit approaches; a single gate is insufficient when another approach remains. Assess those conditions rather than accepting a list of design qualities.
3. Use the [decision frame](../references/methods/comparison.md#frame-the-question) to distinguish failed hard constraints, unknown prerequisites and preferences. Identify contradictions, unsupported dimensions, incomplete user journeys and conflicts between drawing, schedules and narrative.
4. Trace requirement claims through [requirements](../references/requirements.md#select-a-jurisdiction-module) and the actual jurisdiction's implemented [module](../references/normative/index.md). Check the owning record, external source fragment and actual project trigger; a registry badge or bibliography does not prove a design requirement. Identify unsupported source selections separately from design failures. Unknown jurisdiction or missing module coverage leaves dependent conclusions open while independent concept work can still be assessed.
5. Inspect requested visual artifacts through [visual delivery](../references/methods/visual-delivery.md); verify geometry/labels/relationships and stated precision, not only file existence or export success.
6. When supplied/performed calculations support the reviewed claim, inspect them against [their contract](../references/calculators.md). A qualitative local critique does not need the calculator contract merely to complete Review. Verify data, method limits, actual run evidence and interpretation. Require an independent check where a material decision depends on uncertain output.
7. Return prioritized actionable findings with location, cause/consequence, evidence and a concrete architectural correction. When the task requests redesign, develop the corrected arrangement and recheck its knock-on effects; a finding list alone is insufficient. Preserve fixed decisions and distinguish blockers from refinements.

For a requested final issue or completed revision, test [evidence adequacy](../references/methods/completion.md#derive-evidence-for-the-requested-use)
and [revision closure](../references/methods/completion.md#close-the-revision-and-state-remaining-conditions)
against its intended use. Reconstruct schedule membership and dimension/property
meaning when those support the result; a stale retired row or unsupported rating
can survive matching identifiers. State the consequence of each open item rather
than treating all unknowns as either harmless or a reason to halt independent work.

For a Russian revision, test [use/works](../references/normative/ru/applicability.md#classify-use-and-works-before-applying-a-rule) and the affected [route/level connections](../references/normative/ru/applicability.md#resolve-entrances-routes-and-mixed-uses). Check the actual drawing and source fragment independently: a scope trigger cannot pass an entrance or exit, and a coherent provisional plan cannot establish a legal work category or consent.

When a style or named precedent supports the proposal, use
[evidence before classification](../references/composition/styles/precedent-transfer.md#read-an-object-before-classifying-it)
to check attribution, actual inspected media and observation versus inference.
Test the [transferred relationship](../references/composition/styles/precedent-transfer.md#transfer-a-mechanism-into-a-changed-brief)
against the new brief and its physical consequences; resemblance alone does not
establish use, performance, source authenticity or image-reproduction permission.

## Result

A clear review outcome with supported findings, retained strengths, corrections and unresolved evidence. Review does not accept/close an Issue or authorize model changes; those actions follow their applicable task contracts.
