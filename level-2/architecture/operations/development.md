# Development

## Inputs

Use the selected concept, requested design stage, accepted geometry/program, relevant project sources and the bounded change or refinement. Identify the baseline before editing.

## Procedure

1. Define what may change and which accepted decisions remain fixed. For a local revision, trace only affected adjacencies, routes, dimensions, facade interfaces and performance assumptions.
2. Refine spatial organization, circulation, openings, facade rhythm and material/assembly intent to the requested stage. Keep area definitions and drawing precision consistent.
3. Read the applicable [program](../references/program-and-space.md), [composition](../references/composition-and-style.md) and [performance/coordination](../references/performance-and-coordination.md) sections.
4. Reserve architectural interfaces with structural and building-services work: zones, shafts, access/maintenance, plant, ceiling/roof depths and facade junctions. State who must confirm them; do not perform unsupported sizing.
5. Reconcile plans, sections/elevations, schedules and written decisions. An illustrative image cannot silently establish dimensions or a construction assembly.
6. Recheck consequences of each change against fixed constraints and selected requirements. Where a conflict appears, identify the exact decision needed rather than broadening the redesign.
7. Update requested 2D visuals and inspect them using [presentation](presentation.md). State remaining coordination and stage limits.

## Result

Provide the developed or revised solution, changed decisions, affected interfaces, evidence and unresolved work. Preserve unaffected baseline decisions; leave model authoring to a separate explicitly requested task.
