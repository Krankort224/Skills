# Bounded revision and uncertainty

Select this method for its question; reuse accepted project facts and decisions. Numerical examples are hypothetical, not area standards. Apply [requirements](../requirements.md) only to the actual jurisdiction and use; demand does not establish physical fit or compliance.

## How should a bounded revision or comparison be judged?

Identify the accepted baseline, requested change, affected rooms, relationships,
flows, totals and interfaces. Preserve accepted facts and decisions outside that
boundary; do not reset the brief. Trace consequences through the schedule and
routes, and identify any new conflict or decision required. Recalculate affected
subtotals on the original area basis and mark uncertain consequences as such.

For a comparison, apply the same scenario, units, boundaries and criteria to
all options. First identify failed or unverified hard constraints, then compare
preferences such as route quality, privacy, daylight opportunity, adaptability,
operational burden and area. Explain qualitative judgments and any weights rather
than hiding a hard failure in a weighted score. Record assumptions, changes,
trade-offs, the preferred option and who must confirm it. State what evidence
would change the judgment and what remains unresolved; area or adjacency studies
alone do not establish code compliance or structural feasibility.

Use a bounded repair in this order: locate the failure in plan and section; mark
fixed elements (structure, core, facade openings, wet/service interfaces and
accepted boundaries); change only the partitions, assignments, access or settings
allowed by the request; then propagate the change to immediate neighbors and
affected floors. Try a door relocation before a larger room, a mix change before
moving a core, and a buffer or route change before assuming an engineered solution.
These are low-disruption candidates, not a compulsory ranking: a door move cannot
repair insufficient frontage or structure through an activity field. If the
requested capacity needs a fixed element to move, report the conflict and offer
explicit alternatives outside the permitted boundary rather than quietly moving it.

## Communicate conditions and remaining decisions

Keep supplied requirements, provisional dimensions/system envelopes, performed
geometry/arithmetic and design judgments distinct. Show a retained solution and
its rejected alternative, the conflict found, the revision made and the specific
unknown capable of overturning it. Give each reservation a location and included
components, not just an area total. Keep project facts and accepted decisions in
the project's existing authoritative material; do not invent a parallel register.
Use [program and space](../program/index.md), [requirements](../requirements.md)
and [calculators](../calculators.md) for their respective decisions and limits.
