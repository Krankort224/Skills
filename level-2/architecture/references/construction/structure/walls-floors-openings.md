# Walls, floors and openings

Use the affected question to connect a room layout to its actual supporting
walls, floor direction and openings. This reference develops architectural
organization and reservations; it does not select reinforcement, member sizes,
fasteners or a certified assembly. Use the existing
[envelope functions](../envelope/assemblies.md#assign-control-functions-before-drawing-layer-order)
and [section budget](../../space/vertical/section-and-services.md#budget-the-section-and-routes)
rather than reconstructing their contracts here.

## Make wall roles and floor direction agree

Mark each wall as gravity-bearing, lateral-resisting, enclosure/infill or
partition, allowing combined roles. A material name cannot establish that role:
a masonry leaf can be nonbearing, while a stud wall can support a floor. Place
the supports of the upper plan on the lower plan. A wall over an open shop,
entrance or communal room needs an actual transfer strategy or a revised plan.
Calling the upper wall a party wall does not make its weight disappear.

Compare these organizations against the same rooms, openings and future change:

| Organization | Architectural opportunity | Interface that can reverse the choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated bearing walls with one-way floor units/joists between them | Support lines can coincide with repeated rooms or dwelling boundaries; perpendicular walls may be freer for frontage and openings. | The span direction fixes which lines need bearings. A long opening, shifted upper wall or stair void may interrupt that support. Retain the real wall thickness, bearing/connection zones and lateral system; a thin finish line is not the wall. |
| Beam-and-column frame with separate infill/partitions | A ground common room and upper cellular layout can have different divisions; facade infill need not carry upper floors. | Columns, beams, stability bays and deflection allowances still occupy space. Heavy partitions need an accepted load arrangement. A later partition is not automatically allowed anywhere; changing infill mass or stiffness can change the structural proposal. |
| Two-way slab on a support grid | A more continuous soffit can ease some room/service arrangements, with less dependence on a single repeated wall direction. | Column regions, edges, drops/capitals where used, deflection and openings require coordination. A flat drawing does not prove a thin slab or permission to put a riser beside any column. |

Then choose a physical floor family. Precast units imply a product module,
bearings, joints, transport/lifting and an erection sequence; irregular edges
and large voids may need special units or a different local construction.
Site-cast concrete can develop an irregular edge or opening, but needs planned
reinforcement, formwork/shoring and construction access. Joists permit deliberate
framing around openings, but hangers, trimmed members, bridging and any service
crossings need real zones. An open web is not unrestricted space for cutting.
Compare mass, vibration, impact/airborne sound, fire separation, wet exposure,
finish and replacement as properties of the developed assembly, not a material
ranking. No generic span/depth ratio, bearing length or product capacity is
adopted by this method.

## Place a floor opening without erasing its support

Locate the required void, the actual stair/shaft and its landing or maintenance
access separately. An empty rectangle proves neither a usable stair nor a route
to it. Overlay the void on the floor-unit direction, supporting lines, upper
walls and services. Identify the members/reinforcement interrupted and where
their reactions would go after the change.

Compare moving the void between intact support lines; rotating its long
dimension to reduce the number of interrupted members; a designed local frame
around it; or changing the affected structural bay. These are proposals to
develop, not interchangeable fixes. In joist framing, interrupted members may
deliver to headers/trimmers and then to real supports; that diagram does not
license copying a timber detail into a prestressed concrete slab. In precast
or prestressed work, coordinate unit layout and approved openings with the
manufacturer/engineer before fabrication or cutting. The apparent size of a
hole, or its position over a visible hollow core, grants no cutting permission.

Follow the new edge through section: beam depth, headroom below, stair headroom,
guarding, edge protection, acoustic/fire separation and the service route.
Temporary support and construction sequence are part of the proposal when
existing structure is affected. If a frame around a void introduces a column
into the entrance below, the architectural repair is incomplete. Reconcile the
actual flight/landing geometry and applicable requirements independently of
the structural opening.
On each level, check the landing-to-door route against retained walls/supports;
an upper landing can be blocked by a wall that does not obstruct the lower route.
For a stair, test its [walking profile and overhead section](../../space/vertical/stair-section.md#build-and-test-the-walking-profile)
as well as that plan connection; moving the hole alone cannot raise a fixed roof.

## Develop a wall opening as support and use

Keep four geometries distinct: the structural/rough opening, the installed
frame, the glazed/light opening and the clear opening in the selected operating
position. A nominal window width establishes none of the other three. Reserve
installation/tolerance, frame attachment and movement zones using the selected
system, rather than shrinking a masonry opening to the nominal unit size.

Trace loads around the rough opening. A lintel/header must deliver to actual
jambs/piers/posts and their supports below. An outer masonry leaf may need its
own support even when the inner leaf carries the floor. A decorative soldier
course or arch shape is not evidence of a developed lintel/thrust mechanism.
Widening adjacent openings can leave an inadequate pier or remove a stability
line; a smaller opening split by a real support can be a better spatial answer
than a thin unsupported panoramic strip. Retain the desired view and furniture
fit while comparing these options; no lintel table or assumed masonry arching
angle replaces actual load assessment.

Choose opening operation together with use. Fixed glazing preserves a continuous
view but supplies no user-operated opening. A sliding leaf avoids a projecting
swing yet leaves part of the aperture occupied; a hinged or pivoted leaf sweeps
a real internal/external zone. Check furniture, handles, guarding, screens,
shades, cleaning and glass replacement in the intended operating positions.
Separate effective ventilation/airflow from a diagram's nominal open fraction;
neither proves an emergency escape opening or daylight adequacy. Use actual
requirements and system data for those conclusions. Develop rain/air/thermal
transitions through the existing
[opening-junction method](../envelope/assemblies.md#opening-head-jamb-and-sill).

## Let different materials move without losing separation

Compare deformation sources at the actual interface: frame/slab deflection and
creep, masonry moisture/temperature movement, timber moisture change and facade
temperature movement. Do not assume all materials move the same amount or in
the same direction. A rigidly packed nonbearing infill head can unintentionally
take load from a deflecting floor; making it loose without a developed restraint
can instead leave an unstable wall. Reserve a designed movement/restraint
connection and keep its compatible air, acoustic and fire functions continuous.

Place joints with the composition and construction sequence: changes in height,
material, support or restraint are questions to resolve, not a universal spacing
recipe. Keep the intended movement path free of rigid finish bridges, fixings
or service crossings that defeat it. A structural movement joint, a masonry
control joint and a facade seal joint have different jobs; a matching line on
the elevation does not make them equivalent. Existing crack diagrams can prompt
questions about movement/support, but do not diagnose a building from a shape.
Use [compatibility and replaceability](../envelope/assemblies.md#compatibility-movement-and-replaceability)
for the assembly interfaces and maintenance strategy.

## Worked revision of a two-storey learning house

This original hypothetical study fixes an internal 12×18 m shell, floor datum0,
upper finished floor+3.40 and minimum lower occupied height2.60. Bearing-wall
axes x0,6,12 continue down to permitted supports; provisional one-way units span
between these lines. The walls have real thickness, bearings and connections
still to be developed: axis distances are not clear room widths. Trial upper
floor finish0.10, unit depth0.22 and separate service zone0.20 give lower clear
height **3.40−0.10−0.22−0.20=2.88 m**. These are comparison inputs, not sizes
derived from the span.

**Faulty change.** A proposed stair void x5…7/y7…11 cuts both floor bays and the
wall at x6. Erasing the wall segment leaves upper wall/floor reactions unresolved.
Calling the 2×4 m rectangle a compliant stair also omits flights and landings.

**A — retain the bearing organization.** Move the structural void into the east
bay, x7…10/y7…11. The intact x6 wall remains; the 3×4 m void is12 m², compared
with the original8 m². In this bay compare designed headers at y7 and11 spanning
between the x6 and12 support lines, with trimmers at x7 and10 delivering to
them. The actual frame widths, connections and remaining units must be fitted;
these are axis coordinates, not promised clear void dimensions. Replace the
affected unit layout with a coordinated local floor/frame proposal before
fabrication; do not field-cut the initial units to obtain it. Refit the stair,
landing and upper circulation to that reservation rather than leave the old
door in its previous position. The shell's216 m² plan contains12 m² of void and
204 m² before wall/frame/shaft deductions; it is not204 m² of net usable rooms.

**B — retain the central void position.** Replace the affected bearing-wall
organization with a developed frame/transfer proposal, including upper wall
loads and lower support locations. It can preserve the central arrival and
smaller void footprint, but any resulting columns, transfer depth and changed
stability paths belong on both plans. It is not equivalent to removing only
the wall lines at x6. Compare the value of the central circulation with that
structural and spatial change before selecting it.

**Section counterexample.** In A, a trial header envelope0.55 replaces the local
0.22 floor zone. Keeping0.20 services below it yields
**3.40−0.10−0.55−0.20=2.55**, failing the2.60 brief despite the improved plan.
Route those services beside the header in coordinated lower-demand bands and
its underside is+2.75, conditionally preserving2.60 below. Any actual duct,
connection or deflection allowance exceeding that margin reopens the choice.
Do not cut the header to restore an apparently flat ceiling. Capacity, landing
and flight fit and separation of the new void remain unverified in both options.

**Opening and material revision.** The east bay's north window is labelled
“3 m opening.” Retain its view intent but schedule the rough opening, installed
frame, light opening and actual opening mode separately. With floors spanning
in x, the north edge is not their assumed main bearing line; it still needs a
developed facade/wind/support arrangement. Compare fixed central glazing plus
an operable side light against several operable leaves. The first protects the
view/furniture zone but may provide less usable opening; the second needs clear
sweep and reachable handles. Neither receives a ventilation percentage from
the nominal3 m width. Develop supports for backing and any outer leaf, keep
the chosen movement head separate from frame attachment, then continue the
existing head/jamb/sill control paths. A rigid new masonry infill against the
local header would undo the intended nonbearing/movement strategy.

## Source boundary

Selected P05 and ASK03/05/08 pages, identities and exclusions are recorded in
[sources](../../../sources/library.md#architecture-library-selected-content). Source diagrams,
numeric reinforcement/spacing/bearing recipes and generic cutting permission
are not installed. Photos establish visible arrangements, not tested connection
capacity or concealed construction. The worked revision is original, uses
explicit trial inputs and grants no structural or regulatory approval.
