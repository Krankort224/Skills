## Read a coupled decision without loading the corpus

Read the selected method through its next peer heading, including inherited input conditions, examples and failure/correction. A file may contain several coherent section nodes. Follow **Requires** when its stated input/condition is unresolved, **Affects** when the physical or operational consequence is present, **Verified by** for the named claim/check, and **Illustrated by** when the example helps the decision. A link does not execute a check.

Reuse already loaded facts and sections. Reciprocal influence links are legitimate; do not recursively expand every neighbor. Continue until the requested decision and relevant checks are supported or the specific missing evidence is identified. No fixed hop quota may conceal a real dependency. An unconditional prerequisite cycle needs semantic review; a cycle in the authored graph is not itself an error.

Navigation selects questions. Cases illustrate mechanisms with their own observed/reported/inferred limits. Provenance identifies sources and rights; normative records select actual jurisdiction/version authority. Neither source-only records nor a route establish a local method or compliance.

Do not copy a general room procedure into every typology or a layer/removal method into every material. A local reminder of essential conditions is not a second owner.

## Coverage and open gaps


Use the selected bounded method, not its subject label as a completion claim. Quarter, program and the declared building families now have bounded
operational methods; they do not establish every urban-design or building-type
competency. Unexamined specialist typologies remain partial. Bounded methods now
address existing-fabric evidence/intervention, integrated occupied environmental
states, maintenance journeys and equivalent-function quantity/cost reasoning.
Their actual condition, environmental, user, product and specialist inputs still
need project-specific evidence. The style selector declares the selected
direction/object perimeter rather than world-history completeness; unrepresented
traditions need new evidence. Requested-scope completion methods now connect
evidence sufficiency, schedules, precision and final revision closure. Their
readiness judgment applies to the stated use and actual evidence, not every
possible project stage, specialist typology or construction decision.
Those gaps are specific knowledge work, not reasons to read every library file
or execute a whole project as a test. An absent coursework brief is not a gate.

Source-only, unreviewed and inaccessible material must remain distinguishable
at the provenance owner. Missing critical evidence blocks its dependent claim;
do not call it out of scope merely to declare completion. Architectural methods
can inform engineering coordination, model authoring and model-based reviews,
but they do not themselves prove member/MEP sizing, certification, model fidelity
or exchange correctness. Support each such claim with its own evidence and checks;
small method/route checks establish only the behavior actually observed.

**Coverage terms:** Method means a locally usable procedure for its named bounded question; Partial means useful material exists without the claimed breadth or complete decision procedure; Source-only means a locator without a local method; Boundary means a further evidence/check dependency, not a restriction on compatible tools or expertise. Unread or inaccessible material stays explicit at its source owner.
