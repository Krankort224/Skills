# Russian normative catalog

Use this discovery catalog only after Russia is confirmed for the task. It
supports source selection, not a compliance verdict. Follow the selection
procedure in [requirements](requirements.md); read only the relevant thematic
records and the actual provisions needed for the decision. Full normative texts
remain external. Project-specific source selections and requirements belong to
the existing project owner.

## Coverage and date

Evidence checked **2026-11-01**. This is a broad federal architectural discovery
perimeter, with local discovery routes, not an inventory of every Russian act.
The perimeter follows legal/technical, sanitary, environmental, heritage and
object-use branches rather than a target document count. Core registry scope
abstracts, amendment/replacement fields and selected legal provisions were read;
this does not amount to full review of all consolidated documents.

Record verification is intentionally granular: a checked registry scope can
support candidate selection while current amendment content, transition rules
or a particular design clause remain unresolved. Do not promote a historical
agency-hosted commercial consolidation, search excerpt or issuer announcement
into a verified current normative text. A last-checked date describes the
retrieval, not a certification of completeness at that date.

## Select a thematic route

Start with [task-to-record routes](normative-routes.md), which link architectural
questions and material conditions to individual records. Use the thematic
overview below when the question is not covered there; inspect its contents or
headings, then retrieve the selected section rather than the whole group.
Follow the canonical [complete selective reading](requirements.md#complete-selective-reading)
procedure, including recovery of truncated output and every record's limits.

| Architectural question | Read | Conditions that change the selection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work classification, federal safety framework, source/edition mechanism, alteration or project documentation | [Federal framework](normative/federal-framework.md) | New construction, reconstruction, capital repair, premises alteration, approval/expertise stage, project dates and safety route |
| Location, parcel, settlement planning, access, landscape, heritage or protected area | [Territory and special conditions](normative/territory-and-special-conditions.md) | Adopted local text/map, cadastral parcel, land use, zone and establishing act, site hazards, protected status |
| Residential, public, industrial, educational, medical, parking, cultural, sports, mixed-use or hotel space; area definition | [Building typologies](normative/building-typologies.md) | Host and embedded uses, users, height, work type, particular typology exclusions, measurement purpose and appendix |
| Escape, fire resistance/spread, emergency systems and inclusive access | [Fire and accessibility](normative/fire-and-accessibility.md) | Functional fire class, height/depth, occupant capabilities, work/replacement scope, compartment strategy and specific safety route |
| Hygiene, daylight, noise, thermal protection, climate and architectural interfaces with engineering | [Environment and interfaces](normative/environment-and-interfaces.md) | Actual activity, room use, location/climate, new/reconstructed/existing condition, sanitary and technical scope/exclusions |

Do not choose a single building code as a substitute for the other branches.
For example, a public use inside an apartment building needs host/embedded-use
selection, sanitary and fire/accessibility review, plus any consent/alteration
and local-façade conditions. An unknown use cannot be assigned a default fire
class. Reuse known task information; request only missing facts that change the
decision.

has been reviewed. Exact document links and the fragments actually read are in
the thematic records. Check the source's edition date, not merely crawl date or
HTTP success.

## Read a record

Each record owns identity, authority links, scope, edition/status, application
basis, related documents and verification limits. Its heading is the stable
local identifier; external document identifiers retain their official spelling.

An anchor selects the whole record, including its verification limitations.
It does not authorize reading an arbitrary file prefix or assuming that content
stored but not displayed was inspected. Retrieval rules and recovery are owned
by [requirements](requirements.md#complete-selective-reading).

- **Scope checked at registry level:** the official abstract was read; it is
  sufficient for a potential-source route, not every exception or provision.
- **Selected text checked:** identified articles/sections/pages were read at
  the stated edition. Unread amendments and later changes remain open.
- **Discovery only / inaccessible:** do not state operative scope, thresholds,
  replacement effects or applicability beyond the evidence identified.

These evidence levels are separate from the project's selection outcomes
(`applicable`, `potential`, `not applicable`, `unresolved`) in requirements.md.
A currently registered SP is not automatically applicable to the project;
an older edition may need investigation under a project transition rather than
being silently substituted or discarded.

## Unresolved evidence and exclusions

Do not conceal critical source gaps by excluding the theme. In particular,
current sanitary replacements, current legal consolidations/transitions and
current 1417 rules remain incompletely available. One live registry entry has
been read; the second selected entry and wider current state remain unresolved.
These limits block claims of
an entirely verified current normative base and any dependent project
conclusion. The catalog remains useful for discovery, scope screening and
identifying exactly what evidence is needed next.

Detailed nuclear, mining, radiological, offshore, military, civil-defence,
hazardous-process, transport-infrastructure and specialist engineering design
are outside this general architectural increment. When the task identifies
such a trigger, retrieve its special sources and responsible specialist rather
than apply generic civil-building guidance. No complete municipal inventory,
legal opinion, regulatory approval or compliance engine is provided.

## Maintain the catalog

Update the owning record when an official amendment, replacement, cancellation
or contradiction is found. Preserve the relevant historical edition pointer if
project transitions may require it. Record the checked date and exact evidence;
do not refresh dates without checking content. Trace related records when scope
changes, and keep unresolved findings visible in this matrix.

For a project requirement, store the exact selected provision, applicable
edition/change, legal/project basis, date condition, design response and
remaining question in the project's existing source. Do not copy project
requirements or full standards into this shared catalog.
