# Regular, turning and end residential sections

Use this method to transform a recoverable housing section while retaining its
actual household program. A section here is a repeated apartment/core grouping,
not a drawing cut. [Housing/access](access-and-court.md) owns access-family choice;
[dwelling fit](dwelling-fit.md) owns furnished use and deductions. Transform their
actual geometry and interfaces rather than duplicating their procedures.

## Recover the baseline and its interfaces

Freeze the accepted plan/section revision, coordinate datum, wall/clear-area
definitions, floor heights, household mix and required essential activities.
Retain actual rooms/furniture/actions, doors and passage widths, occupied window
fronts, common core/landing, wet rooms/ducts, support/voids and ground entry/roof
routes. Identify fixed versus changeable parts and their authority. Missing
opening, household or construction inputs remain explicit, not silently relaxed.

Tag each boundary segment by physical role: exterior with actual exposure;
connected/party edge; core/common route; dwelling/private boundary; service or
opening. Tag normal direction and available occupied frontage, not just a facade
length total. A window on a connected section edge has no exterior exposure;
an end wall becoming external needs a real enclosure and support decision.
Keep exact apartment/core ownership polygons so every area is counted once.

State what regular/turning/end means in this proposal. A regular group normally
joins the next group along declared edges; a turning group changes that joining
direction; an end group terminates it. Site orientation, angle, neighbor geometry,
section spacing and program determine the actual variant. These are not three
universal plans, nor proof that every section is obtainable by rigid rotation.

## Transform, reconnect and test

1. Transform a selected piece explicitly: rotate/reflect its points and normals,
   then translate. For angle θ, x'=a+x cosθ−y sinθ,
   y'=b+x sinθ+y cosθ. Distances and areas within a rigid piece survive;
   its contact with other pieces, true orientation, light and routes do not.
2. Intersect actual ownership polygons. Any positive-area private/private or
   private/core overlap fails. Test the clear core/room construction inside the
   reservation too; a tiny residual polygon or equal total area cannot house
   the former stair, landing or furnished room.
3. Reconnect **whole door portals**, not one pivot point, to continuous common
   circulation. Trace apartment→landing→stair/lift→actual ground exit and roof
   access at all relevant levels. Test door states, waiting, maintenance and
   support around openings through their existing owners. A second core is a
   new area, ground address, support/roof and operating commitment, not a free
   symbol for repairing a disconnected entrance.
4. Reclassify every joined/external face and occupied opening. At an inner corner
   test the actual receiver, neighboring solid and requested view/sky/sun claim.
   Two nominal fronts can look directly into another wing or another dwelling.
   Move/refit a room/opening, set back the wing, enlarge the court or revise the
   admitted program when authorized. A simple outlook test is not daylight or
   insolation approval; use [direct-sun geometry](../../performance/comfort/multi-building-sun.md)
   and [occupied daylight](../../performance/comfort/daylight-view.md) as required.
5. Overlay wet rooms and individual shaft channels on consecutive floors, ground
   and roof. Retest their actual containment, access and branch crossings after
   the transformation. Check support topology, openings, wall roles and roof
   enclosure where those changed; unchanged room areas do not preserve services.
6. Close the dwelling-type count and common/private/wall/shaft ledger on the
   same boundary. Reconcile footprint, court/service ground and any changed
   housing floor/population basis. Report a larger footprint or fewer homes
   before ranking the option. Retain the failed baseline and the changed variables
   so a later constraint can reopen the correct decision without restarting.

## Carry wet rooms, shafts and ground through levels

Apply the same chosen unit transform to each room, shaft **channel** and branch,
not just the bathroom label. For example, the first kitchen-channel centre
(u8.125,v10.025) is(17.475,10.025) in R's B but(20.025,−1.875) in T's B.
Overlay the next floor's actual coordinates; the old roof terminal/base connection
does not follow automatically. A one-floor rotation or moved wet room requires
a real reroute/shaft opening, support and ceiling reservation, not an invisible
diagonal through a bedroom or another dwelling. Use [natural-exhaust topology](../../performance/comfort/natural-exhaust-topology.md)
for the separate room/transfer/channel/roof chain and its reversed-flow risks.

Reconcile basement services, support/grid/voids, roof outlets/maintenance and
each ground door-to-court path. Adding a separate core instead of extending one
adds its actual floor area, street/roof access, support and operational duties;
recount flats/frontage before claiming it solves the turn. Structural and legal
approval remain independent. Preserve R and the explicit change record so an
end-wall, fixed opening, missing land or shaft conflict reopens its own inputs.
