# Russian task-to-record routes

Use only for a confirmed Russian task. Choose the row matching the decision and
its material conditions; the links are starting records, not a compulsory pack.
Read [requirements](requirements.md#russia-specific-source-selection) for the
shared selection rules and [complete selective reading](requirements.md#complete-selective-reading)
before opening a record. Unknown conditions remain questions; an unknown trigger
must not be treated as absent. For another jurisdiction use the general
requirements procedure, without loading these Russian records by default.

Preserve the accepted plan, area definition and decisions outside the revision.
A residential SP's new-build/reconstruction scope does not automatically settle
its application to every existing-apartment change. Choose additional branches
because of an affected interface, rather than loading every specialist file.

For an unmatched question, inspect the relevant thematic contents/heading list
from the [catalog](normative-catalog.md#select-a-thematic-route), then retrieve

the current record's critical condition requires it. Do not treat this route
index as scope/status evidence; the record and selected authoritative provisions
own those facts.
