Use this as a routing catalog for Russian projects, not a substitute for project-specific legal and technical review. Establish location, use, work type, stage and applicable edition before choosing provisions. Architecture coordinates envelope, openings, spaces and routes with responsible specialists; this catalog does not calculate engineering performance or prescribe a model or output format. See [federal framework](federal-framework.md) for the legal and mandatory-application mechanism.

## Coverage and open work

The Rosstandart SP register and individual cards are primary discovery/status evidence; record scope text is identified below. Regulator notices and explanations are primary evidence of what the regulator announced, not the enacted text. Missing material identity or scope is flagged in the record; detailed provisions are checked when a project decision needs them. Sanitary law, hygiene, climate, acoustics, light, envelope and engineering interface branches are considered; only records with actual evidence are asserted. Check official amendments, transition rules, regional and object-specific requirements at project time. A register card is not an exhaustive consolidated text. External water and sewerage (СП 31, 32), waste (89-ФЗ), energy efficiency (261-ФЗ) and environmental protection (7-ФЗ), sanitary protection zones and sector rules are discovery-only leads, not reviewed for scope or current status. For operations, education, food and transport uses, the regulator’s [2026 notice](https://www.28.rospotrebnadzor.ru/content/1/18130/) identifies new rules including СП 2.1.4284-26 and СП 2.4.2.4283-26; see the partial records below and verify their full scope and current amendments before use. Specialized hazardous/radiological, industrial technological and civil-defense systems are outside this pass and require specialist review. Engineering sizing and calculations are outside this architectural routing catalog.

## sp-50-13330-2024

- **Identity:** СП 50.13330.2024 «Тепловая защита зданий», code of practice approved by Минстрой России, order 327/пр of 15.05.2024.
- **Authority:** [Rosstandart card](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/5081dae9-9ee9-455f-80e8-d093d495361c), “Основные сведения”, “Регистрация в фонде”, “Область применения” and “Ссылочные данные”.
- **Scope:** Thermal protection design for new or reconstructed residential, public, industrial, agricultural and warehouse buildings over the scope's area limit where temperature and humidity conditions must be maintained. Coordinate envelope and openings with thermal specialists. The card excludes worship buildings, certain periodically or seasonally heated buildings, short-lived temporary buildings, greenhouses and refrigerated buildings, cultural heritage and listed engineering-service structures; consult exact conditions. Historic-value buildings have special treatment stated in the card.
- **Edition/status:** Card says effective from 16.06.2024, active, replaces СП 50.13330.2012; no replacement shown. It does not establish that all amendments were reviewed.
- **Application basis:** Conditional selection of SP provisions; see [federal framework](federal-framework.md) and project-specific mandatory lists/transition rules. Do not infer blanket obligation from register status.
- **Related:** climate СП 131; glazing/daylight СП 52; HVAC СП 60; sanitary rules.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; card identity, status, scope, exclusions and replacement read; full text, amendments and legal applicability not verified. Recheck all for project use.
