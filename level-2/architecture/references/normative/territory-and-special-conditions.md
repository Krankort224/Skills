## sp-42-13330-2026

- **Identity:** СП 42.13330.2026 «Градостроительство. Планировка и застройка территорий», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/f6917ab4-63d8-4ecb-9794-0b4990ba3b99), identity, approval, scope and replacement fields.
- **Scope:** The register covers planning of new and existing urban and rural settlements and other municipal and regional territories, including territorial planning, zoning, planning and development and other activities changing the established environment. Use for both new development and alteration at the relevant planning scale; the register is not a parcel-specific approval.
- **Edition/status:** Register says effective from 12 July 2026, approved 11 June 2026 by order 368/пр, replacing СП 42.13330.2016. The earlier edition remains relevant to dated project decisions where applicable; do not assume its provisions are unchanged.
- **Application basis:** Determine the applicable legal regime and specific SP provisions as described in [federal framework](federal-framework.md); scope alone does not make every provision mandatory.
- **Related:** SP 82, SP 396, regional and local planning rules and parcel instruments below.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: register identity, scope, dates and replacement fields read; full text and any transition rules not reviewed. Check actual edition, amendments and project-specific transition dates.

## sp-82-13330-2016

- **Identity:** СП 82.13330.2016 «Благоустройство территорий», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/8d5a8ef5-f450-4356-ac88-72cd17c416cf), scope, dates and amendments; [ФАУ ФЦС text](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/74a47ca5-6f43-42d0-b222-32b7e81f5f11), §§ 4.1–4.2.
- **Scope:** Site-improvement design and elements across planning contexts; § 4.1 relates improvement and site preparation to new construction, reconstruction and demolition; § 4.2 addresses execution and acceptance of paths, access drives, planting and other listed works at diverse site uses. Read the applicable full edition for exclusions.
- **Edition/status:** Register lists in force from 17 June 2017 and amendments 1–3. The linked text is not established as fully consolidated; [amendment 3](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/changesdetails/7be1b5e2-70ce-45d7-88b4-c8a7a381610e) changes many references, including sanitary and planning sources. Recheck all applicable amendments.
- **Application basis:** Apply selected provisions according to [federal framework](federal-framework.md), not a blanket mandatory presumption.
- **Related:** SP 42; current local благоустройство rules and accessibility and sanitary routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: register scope/status and text §§ 4.1–4.2; partial, not a full design review.

## sp-396-1325800-2018

- **Identity:** СП 396.1325800.2018 «Улицы и дороги населенных пунктов. Правила градостроительного проектирования», Минстрой России, code of practice.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/b06a81a0-0143-4299-aac1-764b05cf4242), scope, dates and amendment listing.
- **Scope:** New streets and roads in settlements and reconstruction of existing ones. Consult for a project involving the street network or connections, but do not presume it governs every private access drive; verify the detailed scope and interface.
- **Edition/status:** Register reports effective 2 February 2019; lists amendments 1–3, with no replacement listed. Full amended text and dates not checked.
- **Application basis:** Select applicable provisions using [federal framework](federal-framework.md).
- **Related:** SP 42, SP 82 and transport/accessibility sources.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: register scope and status fields only; project use requires full text and amendments.

## sp-14-13330-2018

- **Identity:** СП 14.13330.2018 «Строительство в сейсмических районах», Минстрой России, code of practice.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/434451e0-4b21-4371-8d57-555efedac3f6), scope, status and amendment list; [amendment 0](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/changesdetails/5b167dc2-dbf5-47a8-b786-7bb660dfdc36), cancellation qualification.
- **Scope:** Register identifies design, construction and reconstruction of buildings and structures on sites with calculated seismic intensity within its stated range, and a separate official procedure above that range. Establish site seismicity from project evidence and check the actual scope before choosing provisions; do not treat a regional label as a site determination.
- **Edition/status:** Register says effective 25 November 2018, replacing 2014; it lists amendments 0–5. Amendment 0 qualifies cancellation of amendment 1; establish the actual applicable text and dates, including any transition, before reliance.
- **Application basis:** Specific provisions under [federal framework](federal-framework.md) and the project's applicable legal basis.
- **Related:** engineering surveys, structure and site hazards.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: register scope/status and amendment 0 summary; no detailed structural provisions reviewed.

## federal-law-73-fz

- **Identity:** Федеральный закон от 25.06.2002 № 73-ФЗ «Об объектах культурного наследия (памятниках истории и культуры) народов Российской Федерации», federal law, Федеральное Собрание РФ.
- **Authority:** [Regional heritage authority's historical text](https://okn.alania.gov.ru/sites/okn/files/media/documents/files/2022-02/%D0%A4%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%D1%82%2025%20%D0%B8%D1%8E%D0%BD%D1%8F%202002%20%D0%B3.%20N%2073-%D0%A4%D0%97.pdf), article 36(1)–(3), pp. 56–57, is a 2021 commercial consolidation hosted by a public authority, not proof of current law.
- **Scope:** The historical text addresses investigations, design, earthworks and construction where registered, identified or potentially heritage objects occur, including work within heritage territory and on directly connected parcels. For new work or alteration, investigate the object, boundaries, protection zones, archaeological evidence and the relevant protection authority's acts before settling layout or preservation measures; current duties and approvals must be verified.
- **Edition/status:** The inspected copy is dated 21 December 2021; later changes and operative text are unresolved. Use [official legal information portal](https://pravo.gov.ru/) to find amendments and current text.
- **Application basis:** Federal legal requirements, subject to their current terms and site facts; no SP status inference.
- **Related:** Parcel restrictions, local heritage authority and its specific boundary/regime acts.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: historical article 36(1)–(3) read; current edition and individual design/approval triggers not verified.

## federal-law-33-fz

- **Identity:** Федеральный закон от 14.03.1995 № 33-ФЗ «Об особо охраняемых природных территориях», federal law, Федеральное Собрание РФ.
- **Authority:** [Роснедра regional office copy](https://cfo.rosnedra.gov.ru/upload/iblock/be9/3udp8msrimpp9uaby21xjnv4ghueunzo.pdf), article 15(1)–(2), pp. 15–16, dated 6 March 2025, reproduces a commercial consolidation.
- **Scope:** The inspected historical article differentiates national-park functional zones and prohibits specified damaging or incompatible activities. A protected area, its category, actual boundary, functional zone and individual regulation must be identified before design. This national-park example does not establish the regime of every protected area or buffer.
- **Edition/status:** Copy is the 8 August 2024 edition with changes effective 1 March 2025 and flags later amendments elsewhere; current law and site-specific regulation require verification via [official portal](https://pravo.gov.ru/).
- **Application basis:** Federal law and the specific protected-area regime, not an automatic building prohibition everywhere.
- **Related:** Environmental law, water and heritage routes, regional protected-area authority.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: historical article 15(1)–(2) read in part; current text and other categories not reviewed.

- **Scope:** Inspected provisions address harmful or potentially harmful activity, territorial planning and zoning, and architectural design, construction, reconstruction and capital repair. Investigate relevant environmental impacts and necessary measures for the actual activity and site. The text alone does not establish that every project requires a particular expert review.

- **Related:** Protected areas, water, ecological assessment and surveys.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: articles 34–36(1) read in part; current consolidation and project-specific review not verified.

## water-code-74-fz

- **Identity:** Федеральный закон от 03.06.2006 № 74-ФЗ «Водный кодекс Российской Федерации», federal code, Федеральное Собрание РФ.
- **Authority:** [Росрыболовство regional office copy](https://prim.fish.gov.ru/upload/iblock/b13/ai8807ndalg72d80yabqcpuyto6ulvpj.pdf), article 65(1)–(3), p. 24, commercial 8 August 2024 edition.
- **Scope:** Water-protection zones adjoin specified water bodies and have a special use regime; coastal protective strips inside them have additional restrictions. Where a site approaches water, establish the actual water body, applicable boundaries, special regime and other water-use authorizations; a map overlay alone does not resolve them.
- **Edition/status:** Inspected edition is 2024; current provisions and special cases remain to be checked against [official portal](https://pravo.gov.ru/).
- **Application basis:** Federal legal restrictions and relevant establishing acts; verify facts and current terms.
- **Related:** Land restrictions, protected areas, drainage and environmental design.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: article 65(1)–(3) read; detailed prohibitions, exclusions and current amendments not verified.

## land-code-136-fz

- **Identity:** Земельный кодекс Российской Федерации от 25.10.2001 № 136-ФЗ, federal code, Федеральное Собрание РФ.
- **Authority:** [Prosecutor's office hosted copy](https://epp.genproc.gov.ru/documents/1889816/58571248/garant_zemelny_kodeks_rf.pdf/234c52a9-f298-93a5-f1e7-f5819f18aa90?download=true&t=1612611589580), article 56(1)–(2), historical commercial text.
- **Scope:** Historical article 56 identifies legal bases for limiting land rights. For the site, obtain land category, permitted use, registered rights and restrictions, and the actual acts creating applicable restrictions; investigate special-use zones independently of zoning. The existence and effect of a restriction must be established from current law and its particular act.
- **Edition/status:** Historical 2020 copy; current edition and amendments not verified. Search [official portal](https://pravo.gov.ru/) and obtain current registry extracts.
- **Application basis:** Federal land law and specific legally established restrictions, not a blanket SP obligation.
- **Related:** PZZ, GPZU, heritage, water, protection and sanitary zones.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: article 56(1)–(2) opening; full current restrictions regime and registration consequences unresolved.

## local-discovery-routes

Begin with cadastral number, boundary and intended use/work. Obtain an up-to-date ЕГРН extract and a GPZU where relevant; compare territorial zone, permitted uses and building parameters with the adopted PZZ text and map, relevant territorial-planning documentation and applicable regional/local design standards. Trace each intersecting heritage, water, sanitary, utility, aviation or other special-use zone to the actual establishing act and authority; check project-specific surveys and conditions. A GPZU or web map is an input, not by itself a complete or conclusive statement of every constraint. General planning-law mechanism is owned by [federal framework](federal-framework.md).

**Moscow worked route.** Start with [Moscow's GISOGD regulation](https://www.mos.ru/upload/documents/files/5111/OGISOGDgMoskvi.pdf), appendix entries 5–6: the inspected text lists PZZ maps, special-use and heritage territory boundaries, amending acts and improvement rules. For the address or cadastral parcel, find its zoning sheet and territorial regulations, then obtain the current adopted [120-ПП PZZ](https://www.mos.ru/upload/documents/files/1313/120_pp_ot_28032017_120_pp_ob_utverzhdenii_pzz.pdf) and all parcel-relevant amendments. Historical §§ 3.1.3, 3.5.2 and 3.5.7 explain that zone boundaries may cross parcels and point to establishing acts. Check GPZU, heritage authority records and zone acts separately. The 2017 PDF is a historical starting point, not a verified current consolidation.

**St Petersburg worked route.** Search excerpts from the city's [official announcement](https://www.gov.spb.ru/press/governor/309021/) identify PZZ as Government resolution of 21 June 2016 № 524 and describe a 2025 update; an [official district page](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/terr/reg_kolpino/obrasheniya-grazhdan/faq/10865/) points to architecture committee PZZ materials at [kgainfo.spb.ru](https://www.kgainfo.spb.ru/). Those pages timed out when opened, so treat these as leads. Find the current adopted text, zoning sheet, amendments and parcel regulation there and through [official publication guidance](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/architecture/documents/new_npa/). Investigate the current city planning standards (resolution № 257), GPZU through КГА, and heritage-zone acts through КГИОП; compare [РГИС information](https://www.gov.spb.ru/gov/otrasl/kio/news/257346/) with the source acts. The latter page also timed out on opening. Current texts and layers were not tested for a named site.

**Other Russian regions.** Identify the municipality and responsible planning authority first. Search its official adopted PZZ and zoning map, regional and local design standards, master plan and applicable planning documentation; obtain GPZU and ЕГРН information, then verify each zone with the relevant authority's act. Use regional heritage and protected-area registers and water/utility authorities for site-specific boundaries and regimes. The [official legal portal](https://pravo.gov.ru/) has federal and regional legal search routes. No comprehensive municipality catalogue or current parcel search was performed.

## coverage-and-unresolved-branches

For climate inputs, use the canonical [SP 131 record](environment-and-interfaces.md#sp-131-13330-2025); site hazards and territorial triggers remain here.

Register scope/status was reviewed for SP 42, 82, 396 and 14; full provisions and mandatory-list membership were not audited. Historical agency-hosted texts were read in part for 73-ФЗ, 33-ФЗ, 7-ФЗ, the Water and Land Codes; their current consolidated text is unresolved. Moscow and St Petersburg materials above are discovery routes, not verified current parcel rules. Permafrost, flood/landslide hazards, airfield restrictions, sanitary protection zones, individual regional/local norms and other protected-area categories remain discovery-only: identify them by actual site, object and survey and retrieve the current authoritative acts. Specialized structural, fire, sanitary and project-documentation topics belong to their respective references. No numeric design thresholds or exhaustive municipal coverage are asserted here.
