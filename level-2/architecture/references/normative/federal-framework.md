# Federal framework and documentation

This catalogue routes Russian architectural decisions to federal instruments and documentation standards. It is evidence for source selection, not a determination that a project complies. An official standards card verifies its stated scope/status; it does not verify every clause or make the whole standard mandatory. Checked 2026-11-01.

## coverage-and-application-mechanism

The reviewed core is building-safety law, the 2024 change in justification mechanisms, project-documentation content, housing alteration/consent routes, and documentation standards. Current code consolidation, register amendments and legacy-list transitions require further project-specific verification; unresolved items remain visible below. Fire, sanitary, accessibility, heritage and environmental legislation has separate topic ownership. Technical-regulation law 184-FZ and standardization law 162-FZ are related mechanisms, not substitutes for reading the applicable building law. Energy-saving law 261-FZ requires a separate check when energy obligations are at issue; it has not been reviewed here. Architectural-activity law 169-ФЗ remains a visible discovery-only record because its primary text could not be retrieved.

| Evidence coverage | Records | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official scope/status metadata read | GOST R 21.101-2026; GOST 21.501-2018 | Candidate selection is supported; body provisions/correction require retrieval before detailed use. |
| Bounded original or official-office-hosted text read, including labeled commercial reproductions | GrK 190-ФЗ; 384-ФЗ; 653-ФЗ with indexed 507-ФЗ amendment; Government 87; ЖК 188-ФЗ with enacted 608-ФЗ amendment | Actual scope/exception/timing provisions read; editions and amendment boundaries are explicit, not an exhaustive current consolidation. |
| Partial official discovery/indexed text | Government 1417 and actual registry entries | Instrument identity plus indexed operator metadata observed; complete current rules and live entry state remain unresolved. |
| Discovery only | 169-ФЗ | Identity/source route retained; substantive applicability not verified. |

For each proposed requirement, establish jurisdiction, building/use, work type, stage and legally relevant dates; read the actual provision and exclusions; identify the applicable edition and legal mechanism; retain the justification route in the project evidence. In the post-2024 framework, distinguish the federal safety obligation from the selected means of substantiation. A register entry is not by itself a finding that every provision applies to this building, nor a statement that the entire SP/GOST is mandatory. Legacy Government 815 lists must not be presented as a universal current mandatory catalogue. Where a transition depends on an earlier GPZU or planning document, establish its facts and read the current transition text before choosing an edition.

## grk-190-design-and-expertise

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 190-ФЗ, «Градостроительный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Primorye state-expertise hosted code](https://primgosexpert.ru/?dl_name=190_fz.pdf), ConsultantPlus reproduction with amendment list through 31.07.2025 No. 353-ФЗ: article 1(10), (10.2), (13)–(14.4), article 48(1)–(3.1), (13), (15), article 49(1)–(3.3), (5), (5.2), and 57.4 read on PDF pages 3–4, 137–138, 142–143, 149–150, 154–155 and 329. [Official text-bank route](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090643) timed out. The deferred-date amendment is documented in `fz-653-building-safety-reform`.
- **Scope:** Classify capital/noncapital work and construction/reconstruction/capital repair under article 1 before using article 48's project/working-documentation routes. Article 48(3) exempts specified individual-house/garden-house/garage/outbuilding cases, with conditions and the estimate-check exception in 3.1. Article 49(2)–(3.3) distinguishes expertise exclusions, pipeline-zone/mass-occupancy exceptions and estimate-verification cases; do not infer exemption from size/use alone. Article 57.4 establishes the public register with state-secret limits.
- **Edition/status:** Bounded post-2024 body evidence in the 31.07.2025 reproduction; later amendments through 01.11.2026 have not been exhaustively compared. Its note scheduling the 49(5.2) change for 01.03.2026 is superseded by 507-ФЗ article 8's evidenced deferral to 01.03.2027; do not use that stale editorial note.
- **Application basis:** Federal procedural law. Article 49(5.2) makes the applicable requirements date depend on object type, GPZU/planning-document dates, submission timing and prior positive expertise; read the whole current part and transition rules for the actual dates. Register formation alone does not decide project applicability.
- **Related:** `government-87-project-documentation`; `fz-384-building-safety`; `fz-653-building-safety-reform`; `government-1417-requirements-register`; territorial planning/GPZU/PZZ routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; the listed actual classification, stage, expertise-exception and timing provisions were read in the dated code reproduction. Other article 49 branches and the complete 2025–2026 amendment chain were not reviewed. Verify current affected provisions before determining a project's documentation/expertise route; no project-specific exemption is concluded here.

## zhk-188-alterations

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 188-ФЗ, «Жилищный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [608-ФЗ official publication 0001202312190009](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202312190009), identified but inaccessible; [federal Human Rights Commissioner's hosted ConsultantPlus reproduction of 608-ФЗ](https://ombudsmanrf.org/storage/74a0484f-7d5a-4fe4-883d-a1b5ba1dd5f8/Digest%20-%202023/%D0%98%D0%AE%D0%9D%D0%AC/1/%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA/%D1%82%D1%83%D1%80%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F/51%D0%A4%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%D1%82%2019%20%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%B1%D1%80%D1%8F%202023%20%D0%B3.%20%E2%84%96%20608-%D0%A4%D0%97.pdf), article 1(2)–(3) and article 3 read on PDF pages 2 and 4. [MChS institution-hosted code copy](https://noginsky-sc.organizations.mchs.gov.ru/uploads/document/2022-06-23/84c3fea9558c20459f0b8f62c2af981d.pdf), ConsultantPlus edition 02.12.2019: printed pages 28–33, 38–39, 42 read, articles 25–29, 36(1)–(4), 40. [Current official text-bank route](https://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090645) timed out.
- **Scope:** Alteration/replanning of premises in apartment buildings; article 26 approval/project route, article 27 refusal, article 28 completion, article 29 unauthorized work. Common-property interfaces are separate: historical articles 36(3), 40(2) require all owners' consent for reduction/attachment in their stated circumstances. Do not generalize this into a rule that every interior alteration needs all owners' consent. Determine actual property boundaries and work classification before selecting the route.
- **Edition/status:** The 19.12.2023 No. 608-ФЗ text, read in that office-hosted commercial reproduction, replaces article 25(2) and article 28 from 01.04.2024. Replanning includes boundary/area changes, creation of premises and internal-layout changes even without boundary/area changes. Article 28 distinguishes approval of the acceptance act for completion of alteration from EGRN/cadastral-registration completion for replanning, and requires the notification/technical-plan route in its stated circumstances. Article 3(2) applies the amended rules to specified works completed after commencement under earlier approval/conversion decisions. Current exhaustive consolidation and later amendments remain unverified.
- **Application basis:** Federal housing procedure and property/consent rights within its apartment-building scope. A room-layout drawing does not itself authorize works. Coordinate municipal procedure, building safety, heritage and registration requirements; separate reconstruction from housing replanning on actual facts.
- **Related:** `grk-190-design-and-expertise`; `fz-169-architectural-activity`; residential-building and heritage records. Nonresidential premises inside apartment buildings must not be excluded merely because they are not apartments; other object types need their own legal route.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; 608-ФЗ article 1(2)–(3)/3 actual reproduced text read, resolving the old definition/completion reading gap; code articles 26–27/29/36/40 remain supported by the dated 2019 copy. Verify their current consolidation and local procedure before deciding authorization, consent or completion for a particular project.

## fz-384-building-safety

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 30.12.2009 No. 384-ФЗ, «Технический регламент о безопасности зданий и сооружений»; federal technical regulation, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Primorye state-expertise hosted law](https://primgosexpert.ru/?dl_name=384_fz.pdf), ConsultantPlus reproduction listing 25.12.2023 No. 653-ФЗ as the latest amendment: article 3(1)–(4), 6(1)–(6) and 15(5)–(7) read on PDF pages 4, 6–7 and 10–11. [653-ФЗ original text, FAU FCC mirror](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/e49eeab0-b50d-4ceb-b203-5525c32535a3), article 1 changes to those provisions read as amending evidence.
- **Scope:** Buildings/structures of any purpose, their networks/systems and lifecycle processes, including surveys, design, reconstruction, capital/current repair and demolition. Article 3(3) excludes functional technological-process safety itself while retaining its hazardous effects on the building; article 3(4) adds specialized-facility regimes. The scope is evidenced in the post-2024 reproduction, not inferred from the title.
- **Edition/status:** Actual consolidated post-reform provisions read in the 25.12.2023 amendment edition; no exhaustive later-amendment/status check through 01.11.2026. The older list-based article 6 is not the mechanism reproduced here; any legacy use needs the transition check.
- **Application basis:** Federal safety obligations plus selected justification under articles 6 and 15. Article 6(2) connects national-standard/SP requirements with registry inclusion; article 15(5.1)–(7) requires identified law/supporting provisions and substantiated, approved results with design inputs considered. Choice subject to the assignment does not dispense with justification. See `fz-653-building-safety-reform` and `government-1417-requirements-register`.
- **Related:** GrK 190-ФЗ; fire law 123-ФЗ; accessibility, sanitary and performance topic records. These interfaces do not authorize replacing their separate legal checks.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; listed scope/exclusion and justification provisions read in an official institution-hosted reproduction. The complete current consolidation and any applicable transition remain project-use checks. No whole-SP mandatory status or compliance conclusion follows from this record.

## fz-653-building-safety-reform

- **Authority:** [Official-publication record 0001202312250053](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202312250053), publication pointer identified; [original law text hosted by FAU FCC](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/e49eeab0-b50d-4ceb-b203-5525c32535a3), articles 1, 2, 4, 5 read; [FAU FCC explanation](https://faufcc.ru/deiatelnost/stu), justification/register sections read. [507-ФЗ official-publication record 0001202512280019](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202512280019) identified but timed out; indexed actual article 8 and article 11(1) read in a [Federal Treasury-hosted ConsultantPlus reproduction of 507-ФЗ](https://murmansk.roskazna.gov.ru/storage/page-files/55224/28_12_2025%20N%20507-%D0%A4%D0%97.pdf), saved 09.04.2026, printed page 6; direct PDF open returned a gateway error. Agency explanation is interpretive context, not enacted text.
- **Scope:** Article 1 replaces 384-ФЗ article 6 and adds article 15(5.1), (5.2): justification must identify the law and selected supporting provisions; the designer selects permitted documents/methods subject to the design assignment. National standards/SP requirements use the register mechanism; international/foreign and organizational standards have their own registration conditions. - **Edition/status:** General commencement is 01.09.2024. No exhaustive consolidation through 01.11.2026 is certified here.
- **Application basis:** Amending law governing 384-ФЗ justification and GrK register arrangements. Original article 4 contains an earlier-GPZU/planning-document transition; use its current text and project dates before invoking legacy rules.
- **Related:** `fz-384-building-safety`; `government-1417-requirements-register`; GrK article 57.4. Fire-safety substantiation retains its separate 123-ФЗ regime.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; original reform scope and 507-ФЗ article 8/11(1) indexed amendment fragments read in the named Treasury-hosted commercial reproduction. Earlier-GPZU/planning-document transition is evidenced by original article 4; any subsequent transition amendment and the full current consolidation still need checking. ## government-87-project-documentation

- **Identity:** Government of the Russian Federation Resolution of 16.02.2008 No. 87, «О составе разделов проектной документации и требованиях к их содержанию»; Government resolution approving the associated Regulation.
- **Authority:** [Primorye state-expertise hosted copy](https://primgosexpert.ru/wp-content/files/87_pprf.pdf), ConsultantPlus reproduction, edition 21.10.2025: resolution paragraph 8, Regulation paragraphs 1–4 including 3(1)–3(4), and 13 read on PDF pages 3–6 and 14–16 (one-based PDF pages). [Rosrybolovstvo territorial-agency hosted text](https://tmn.fish.gov.ru/upload/iblock/20d/2sje8p9nfddnaokapk2p8atonn1hdjtg.pdf), edition 03.10.2025, supplies the earlier comparison text for those provisions and paragraph 5. These are dated hosted reproductions, not proof of current official consolidation.
- **Scope:** Paragraph 1 addresses project-documentation sections subject to GrK article 49 expertise for construction, reconstruction and capital repair, including linear facilities and individual stages. Paragraph 2 separates production, nonproduction and linear objects. Paragraph 3 describes text/graphics and coordinated solutions; 3(1) lists non-linear construction sections, including «Объемно-планировочные и архитектурные решения». Paragraph 3(3) makes reconstruction/capital-repair section selection depend on actual works and the assignment; paragraph 4 distinguishes working documentation.
- **Edition/status:** Scope/content in dated edition 21.10.2025 paragraphs 1–4 and 13 agrees with the 03.10.2025 copy. Resolution paragraph 8 states operation until 01.09.2028; that is the dated text's sunset provision, not proof that no amendment or early repeal occurred by 01.11.2026. Complete current consolidation is unverified.
- **Application basis:** Federal project-documentation content regulation within its stage/object/expertise scope; identify current GrK route and relevant paragraphs before determining required deliverables. It does not prescribe a universal format for early concepts.
- **Related:** GrK articles 48–49; `gost-r-21-101-2026`; `gost-21-501-2018`; documentation operation. The standards address documentation rules, while this resolution controls applicable project-documentation composition.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; actual 21.10.2025 paragraphs 1–4 and 13 read and compared with the earlier copy; no material difference found in that bounded comparison. Paragraph 13 covers architectural/spatial justification, facades/interiors/finishes, daylight/insolation, acoustic protection, room programme and relevant drawings with conditional items. Detailed review of other sections or the full amendment chain was not performed; confirm later changes and the project's relevant sections before claiming a submission complete.
