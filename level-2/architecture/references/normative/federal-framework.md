# Federal framework and documentation

This catalogue routes Russian architectural decisions to federal instruments and documentation standards. It is evidence for source selection, not a determination that a project complies. An official standards card verifies its stated scope/status; it does not verify every clause or make the whole standard mandatory. Checked 2026-11-01.

## coverage-and-application-mechanism

Coverage is federal design/expertise procedure, building-safety justification, housing alteration/consent routes, project-documentation composition and documentation standards. Links below lead to the particular instrument's official publication/text-bank record, issuer publication, or primary register. A public office hosting a commercial reproduction does not make that copy a primary source; detailed claims supported only by such copies have been withdrawn. A notice is primary evidence for its own explanation, not for the underlying law's operative text. Fire, sanitary, accessibility, heritage and environmental legislation has separate topic ownership. Laws 184-ФЗ/162-ФЗ are related mechanisms; energy-saving law 261-ФЗ is an additional route when the task concerns energy obligations, not a reviewed record here.

| Evidence coverage | Records | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official scope/status metadata read | GOST R 21.101-2026; GOST 21.501-2018 | Candidate selection is supported; body provisions/correction require retrieval before detailed use. |
| Live operator text and indexed primary-register metadata | Government 1417 route and selected registry entries | Requirement ID 152316 live provision/version/status read; ID 152314 remains indexed-only. This does not verify the Government rules. |
| Instrument identified; primary scope/status text unavailable | GrK 190-ФЗ; ЖК 188-ФЗ; 169-ФЗ; 384-ФЗ; 653-ФЗ; Government 87 | Specific primary routes retained. General subject is identified; affected operative scope, exceptions and status remain explicitly unverified. |

These records support source discovery and selection. A register entry or active standard card does not establish that every provision applies to a building or make a whole SP/GOST mandatory. Legacy Government 815 lists are not a universal current mandatory catalogue. For a later decision, check the provision, exceptions, relevant dates and any transition actually triggered by the project; reading every clause or reconstructing every amendment is not a catalogue-wide completion condition.

Reading routes are article 1 classifications, 48 design, 49 expertise and 57.4 register. Current work/stage conditions and important exclusions were not verified in primary text; the withdrawn reproduction does not establish an exemption for individual housing, repair, small objects or other cases.
- **Edition/status:** Current operative edition and the date-dependent article 49(5.2) position are unverified in the linked primary sources. No 2026/2027 effective-date conclusion is carried over from a commercial reproduction.
- **Application basis:** Federal procedural-law candidate. Confirm the relevant classification or procedure before a decision depends on it; register inclusion alone does not determine project applicability.
- **Related:** `government-87-project-documentation`; `fz-384-building-safety`; `fz-653-building-safety-reform`; `government-1417-requirements-register`; territorial planning/GPZU/PZZ routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; specific primary records identified, content access unavailable. Primary scope/exception verification is an outstanding discovery gap. Detailed article 49 timing or an exemption needs a targeted primary provision check only when a decision turns on it; no full-code/full-chain gate is imposed.

## zhk-188-alterations

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 188-ФЗ, «Жилищный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Official code text-bank record](https://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090645), timed out; [19.12.2023 No. 608-ФЗ official publication 0001202312190009](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202312190009), identified but inaccessible. The 608-ФЗ record is the primary route for that amending act, not a replacement for the code record.
- **Scope:** Russian housing-code candidate for alterations/replanning of premises in apartment buildings and common-property interfaces. Reading routes: articles 25–29 for classification/approval/completion, 36/40 for common property. The exact current boundary between works, exclusions, consent triggers and completion conditions is not primary-verified here. Do not infer consent or exemption from a proposed room layout.
- **Edition/status:** Current code status and the operative changes/commencement of 608-ФЗ have not been read in primary text. Detailed definitions and completion dates derived only from reproduced text are not retained as verified normative facts.
- **Application basis:** Federal housing procedure and property/consent rights within its apartment-building scope. A room-layout drawing does not itself authorize works. Coordinate municipal procedure, building safety, heritage and registration requirements; separate reconstruction from housing replanning on actual facts.
- **Related:** `grk-190-design-and-expertise`; `fz-169-architectural-activity`; residential-building and heritage records. Check whether the relevant provision includes nonresidential premises inside an apartment building; other object types need their own scope check.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; particular primary code/amendment records identified, scope/status content unavailable. This discovery gap remains visible. A later alteration/consent decision needs the relevant current primary provision and local procedure, not a blanket audit of every housing-code amendment.
