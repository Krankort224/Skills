# Federal framework and documentation

This catalogue routes Russian architectural decisions to federal instruments and documentation standards. It is evidence for source selection, not a determination that a project complies. An official standards card verifies its stated scope/status; it does not verify every clause or make the whole standard mandatory. Checked 2026-11-01.

## coverage-and-application-mechanism

The reviewed core is building-safety law, the 2024 change in justification mechanisms, project-documentation content, housing alteration/consent routes, and documentation standards. Current code consolidation, register amendments and legacy-list transitions require further project-specific verification; unresolved items remain visible below. Fire, sanitary, accessibility, heritage and environmental legislation has separate topic ownership. Technical-regulation law 184-FZ and standardization law 162-FZ are related mechanisms, not substitutes for reading the applicable building law. Energy-saving law 261-FZ requires a separate check when energy obligations are at issue; it has not been reviewed here. Architectural-activity law 169-ФЗ remains a visible discovery-only record because its primary text could not be retrieved.

| Evidence coverage | Records | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official scope/status metadata read | GOST R 21.101-2026; GOST 21.501-2018 | Candidate selection is supported; body provisions/correction require retrieval before detailed use. |
| Partial primary/official-hosted text read | 384-ФЗ; original 653-ФЗ; Government 87; ЖК 188-ФЗ | Actual bounded scope evidence exists; no exhaustive current consolidation claimed. |
| Partial official discovery/indexed text | GrK 190-ФЗ; Government 1417 | Specific fragments/mechanism identified; current complete procedural text remains unresolved. |
| Discovery only | 169-ФЗ | Identity/source route retained; substantive applicability not verified. |

For each proposed requirement, establish jurisdiction, building/use, work type, stage and legally relevant dates; read the actual provision and exclusions; identify the applicable edition and legal mechanism; retain the justification route in the project evidence. In the post-2024 framework, distinguish the federal safety obligation from the selected means of substantiation. A register entry is not by itself a finding that every provision applies to this building, nor a statement that the entire SP/GOST is mandatory. Legacy Government 815 lists must not be presented as a universal current mandatory catalogue. Where a transition depends on an earlier GPZU or planning document, establish its facts and read the current transition text before choosing an edition.

## grk-190-design-and-expertise

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 190-ФЗ, «Градостроительный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Official text-bank route](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090643) identified through an official Rosnedra service page but timed out. [North Ossetia–Alania heritage committee-hosted code](https://okn.alania.gov.ru/documents/819), published on that site 02.02.2022: indexed article 48(1), (2.1), (3) fragments read; page/body open timed out. [Original 653-ФЗ](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/e49eeab0-b50d-4ceb-b203-5525c32535a3), article 2(2), (5) read as amending evidence for article 49(5.2) and new 57.4.
- **Scope:** Article 48 routes preparation of project and working documentation for capital-construction objects/parts and contains exceptions; the historical indexed article 48(3) identifies individual-house/garden-house exceptions, whose current conditions require checking. Article 49 is the expertise gateway: its exceptions and public/state expertise triggers must be read before deciding whether review is required. New article 57.4 defines the public requirements register, subject to state-secret limits, and connects its entries to article 49(5) documents.
- **Edition/status:** Only historical indexed article 48 fragments and original 2023 amendments verified. Later 2024–2026 changes and complete current articles 48/49/57.4 are not verified. In particular, original 653-ФЗ article 2(2)'s deferred change to 49(5.2) must not be treated as already operative solely from its original commencement date.
- **Application basis:** Federal procedural law; use current object/work/expertise exceptions, document dates and transition provisions. Register formation does not decide the applicability of every listed provision to a project.
- **Related:** `government-87-project-documentation`; `fz-384-building-safety`; `fz-653-building-safety-reform`; `government-1417-requirements-register`; territorial planning/GPZU/PZZ routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; partial official indexed-fragment and amendment evidence, not full current code scope/status verification. Recheck complete current articles 48, 49, 57.4 and relevant effective-date rules; do not infer mandatory expertise or exemption from this record alone.

## zhk-188-alterations

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 188-ФЗ, «Жилищный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [MChS institution-hosted code copy](https://noginsky-sc.organizations.mchs.gov.ru/uploads/document/2022-06-23/84c3fea9558c20459f0b8f62c2af981d.pdf), ConsultantPlus edition 02.12.2019: printed pages 28–33, 38–39, 42 read, articles 25–29, 36(1)–(4), 40. [Current official text-bank route](https://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090645) timed out. [Rosreestr letter hosted by Roskadastr](https://roscadastre.ru/html/docs/2025/00-19316.pdf) provides an indexed excerpt citing article 26(1) and updated article 28(1); full PDF extraction had no readable lines, so it is supporting discovery, not a fully read amendment.
- **Scope:** Alteration/replanning of premises in apartment buildings; article 26 approval/project route, article 27 refusal, article 28 completion, article 29 unauthorized work. Common-property interfaces are separate: historical articles 36(3), 40(2) require all owners' consent for reduction/attachment in their stated circumstances. Do not generalize this into a rule that every interior alteration needs all owners' consent. Determine actual property boundaries and work classification before selecting the route.
- **Edition/status:** Historical scope/consent provisions read; not current consolidation. The 2019 article 25(2) definition and article 28 completion wording are superseded leads: 19.12.2023 No. 608-ФЗ changed them from 01.04.2024 according to secondary consolidation/discovery. Its enacted primary amendment and any later changes still require verification. Do not reuse the historical technical-passport-only definition or completion procedure.
- **Application basis:** Federal housing procedure and property/consent rights within its apartment-building scope. A room-layout drawing does not itself authorize works. Coordinate municipal procedure, building safety, heritage and registration requirements; separate reconstruction from housing replanning on actual facts.
- **Related:** `grk-190-design-and-expertise`; `fz-169-architectural-activity`; residential-building and heritage records. Nonresidential premises inside apartment buildings must not be excluded merely because they are not apartments; other object types need their own legal route.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; partial dated scope evidence. Exact current articles 25–29/36/40, 608-ФЗ text, later amendments and local approval procedure remain to be checked. No present-day authorization or consent conclusion is made from the old copy.

[ConsultantPlus index](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_8344/) and [Garant metadata](https://base.garant.ru/10106500/) are secondary discovery leads only.
- **Scope:** Discovery-only candidate for professional architectural activity, architectural-project/object changes and relations with architecture authorities. Read current articles 1–3, 12–15, 20–22 before relying on specific duties, author consent or approval relationships; no current scope clause was verified in primary text here.
- **Edition/status:** Secondary metadata reports amendments through 10.07.2023 No. 297-ФЗ. Current effective edition/repeals not verified from a primary source. Historical licensing/copyright chapters must not be imported from an old copy without checking their repeal and Civil Code interfaces.
- **Application basis:** Potential federal legal/contractual duties, subject to current text and actual scope. It does not substitute for GrK/local architectural appearance, heritage or permit checks.
- **Related:** `grk-190-design-and-expertise`; documentation/review operations; heritage and intellectual-property interfaces where triggered.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; discovery only. Primary retrieval blocked; confirm current law and relevant articles before making any normative conclusion.

## fz-384-building-safety

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 30.12.2009 No. 384-ФЗ, «Технический регламент о безопасности зданий и сооружений»; federal technical regulation, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Rosgvardiya-hosted law text](https://www.rosguard.gov.ru/document/article/federalnyj-zakon-ot-30-dekabrya-2009-g--384fz), explicitly edition 02.07.2013 No. 185-ФЗ; articles 1–3 read. Later primary amendment: [653-ФЗ original text, FAU FCC mirror](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/e49eeab0-b50d-4ceb-b203-5525c32535a3), article 1(2), (4), (7), adding/revising articles 3, 6 and 15.
- **Scope:** Buildings/structures of any purpose and their engineering networks/systems; the amended article 3(1) expressly covers surveys, architectural design, construction, reconstruction, capital repair, installation, commissioning, operation/current repair and demolition throughout the lifecycle. The older article 3(3) excludes safety of the functional technological processes themselves, while their hazardous effects on the building remain relevant. Specialized facilities can have additional regimes; their current clauses need separate checking.
- **Edition/status:** Historical 2013 consolidation plus original 2023 amendment read; no exhaustive 2026 consolidation verified. The old article 6 list-based mechanism must not be reused as current without the reform/transition check.
- **Application basis:** Federal technical safety requirements; amended articles 6 and 15 distinguish substantive safety obligations and permitted justification documents/methods. See `fz-653-building-safety-reform` and `government-1417-requirements-register`.
- **Related:** GrK 190-ФЗ; fire law 123-ФЗ; accessibility, sanitary and performance topic records. These interfaces do not authorize replacing their separate legal checks.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; partial scope/status verification. Read Rosgvardiya articles 1–3 and amendment article 1 changes to articles 3/6/15. Recheck current consolidated text, exclusions, transition facts and chosen justification provisions before project use.

## fz-653-building-safety-reform

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 25.12.2023 No. 653-ФЗ, «О внесении изменений в Федеральный закон "Технический регламент о безопасности зданий и сооружений" и отдельные законодательные акты Российской Федерации»; amending federal law, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Official-publication record 0001202312250053](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202312250053), publication pointer identified; [original law text hosted by FAU FCC](https://api.faufcc.ru/api/assets/e49eeab0-b50d-4ceb-b203-5525c32535a3), articles 1, 2, 4, 5 read; [FAU FCC explanation](https://faufcc.ru/deiatelnost/stu), sections on justification documents and the digital register read. Agency explanation is interpretive context, not the enacted text.
- **Scope:** Article 1 replaces 384-ФЗ article 6 and adds article 15(5.1), (5.2): justification must identify the law and selected supporting provisions; the designer selects permitted documents/methods subject to the design assignment. National standards/SP requirements use the register mechanism; international/foreign and organizational standards have their own registration conditions. This is a route-selection mechanism, not permission to omit safety substantiation.
- **Edition/status:** The original law establishes general commencement 01.09.2024 and a separately deferred provision. Its original deferred date must not be treated as current: a later 28.12.2025 No. 507-ФЗ amendment is identified in a [commercial secondary consolidation](https://www.gb-dnr.ru/normative-base/detail/1262), articles 4–5, but the official 507-ФЗ amendment remains to be checked. The portal identifies its operator as ИП Туровец А.В.; it is not an official regional-government source. No exhaustive current consolidation certified here.
- **Application basis:** Amending law governing 384-ФЗ justification and GrK register arrangements. Original article 4 contains an earlier-GPZU/planning-document transition; use its current text and project dates before invoking legacy rules.
- **Related:** `fz-384-building-safety`; `government-1417-requirements-register`; GrK article 57.4. Fire-safety substantiation retains its separate 123-ФЗ regime.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; original reform scope verified, current transitional/deferred-date status partial. Original PDF extraction contains OCR defects, so exact wording must be checked against its facsimile/current official text for a legal conclusion.

## government-1417-requirements-register

- **Identity:** Government of the Russian Federation Resolution of 31.08.2023 No. 1417; original title «Об утверждении Правил формирования и ведения реестра требований, подлежащих применению при проведении экспертизы проектной документации и (или) экспертизы результатов инженерных изысканий, осуществлении архитектурно-строительного проектирования, строительства, реконструкции, капитального ремонта, эксплуатации и сноса объектов капитального строительства, и о признании утратившим силу постановления Правительства Российской Федерации от 12 сентября 2020 г. № 1417»; Government register rules, subsequently amended.
- **Authority:** [Official publication](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202308310014) and [official PDF](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/file/pdf?eoNumber=0001202308310014); original publication identity found, but the retrieved 13-page PDF produced headings only. Original full title corroborated by an indexed [Chelyabinsk municipal procurement announcement](https://zakaz.cheladm.ru/ContentPage.aspx?id=8971); full page open timed out. [Official text-bank pointer](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&link_id=2&nd=605849277&bpa=cd00000&bpas=cd00000&intelsearch=%D3%EA++) found by discovery. [FAU FCC register explanation](https://faufcc.ru/deiatelnost/stu) read in full; it links the [digital register](https://xn--e1ahdbdflckekkc.xn--p1ai/rntd).
- **Scope:** Registry mechanism for identifying requirements across the activities named in the original title; this identity evidence is not inspection of the operative rules. The 653-ФЗ amendment separately connects national-standard/SP application with the register. Actual entry content and applicability to a project's date/work/use must be checked in the register and governing rules.
- **Edition/status:** Partial: original 2023 identity verified; current amended title, clauses and complete amendment chain unresolved. A [2024 regional-ministry amendment PDF lead](https://mstroy.rk.gov.ru/uploads/mstroy/container/2024/08/12/2024-08-12-12-18-09_%E2%84%96%201.pdf) returned a traffic-protection page, not the document. Live register access timed out in this research pass.
- **Application basis:** Procedural register under GrK/384-ФЗ; inclusion is evidence to inspect, not automatic project applicability or whole-document mandatory status. See the mechanism above and `fz-653-building-safety-reform`.
- **Related:** GrK 57.4; legacy Government Resolution of 28.05.2021 No. 815 and its transition/amendment history remain unresolved, not asserted as universally governing.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; partial identity/context evidence, no readable current rules or actual register entry inspected. Before project use retrieve current 1417 text, amendments, relevant entry/provisions, effective dates and legacy-transition conditions. An HTTP-success response containing headings or a protection page is not scope verification. The full-title discovery corrected an earlier shortened search title; the original 2023 title already names design and later lifecycle activities.

## government-87-project-documentation

- **Identity:** Government of the Russian Federation Resolution of 16.02.2008 No. 87, «О составе разделов проектной документации и требованиях к их содержанию»; Government resolution approving the associated Regulation.
- **Authority:** [Rosrybolovstvo territorial-agency hosted text](https://tmn.fish.gov.ru/upload/iblock/20d/2sje8p9nfddnaokapk2p8atonn1hdjtg.pdf), a ConsultantPlus reproduction hosted on an official agency site, edition 03.10.2025; Regulation paragraphs 1–5 including 3(1)–3(4), 4 and 13 read. [Primorye state-expertise hosted copy](https://primgosexpert.ru/wp-content/files/87_pprf.pdf), cover/amendment list read, shows edition 21.10.2025 No. 1623. These are dated hosted reproductions, not proof of current official consolidation.
- **Scope:** Paragraph 1 addresses project-documentation sections subject to GrK article 49 expertise for construction, reconstruction and capital repair, including linear facilities and individual stages. Paragraph 2 separates production, nonproduction and linear objects. Paragraph 3 describes text/graphics and coordinated solutions; 3(1) lists non-linear construction sections, including «Объемно-планировочные и архитектурные решения». Paragraph 3(3) makes reconstruction/capital-repair section selection depend on actual works and the assignment; paragraph 4 distinguishes working documentation.
- **Edition/status:** Actual scope/content read in edition 03.10.2025; a later 21.10.2025 No. 1623 edition is evidenced by the state-expertise copy's cover, but its complete provisions were not compared. Complete currency is unverified. Do not label the historical section list exhaustive for every 2026 project.
- **Application basis:** Federal project-documentation content regulation within its stage/object/expertise scope; identify current GrK route and relevant paragraphs before determining required deliverables. It does not prescribe a universal format for early concepts.
- **Related:** GrK articles 48–49; `gost-r-21-101-2026`; `gost-21-501-2018`; documentation operation. The standards address documentation rules, while this resolution controls applicable project-documentation composition.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; partial current-status verification, actual scope paragraphs and architectural paragraph 13 read. Paragraph 13 covers architectural/spatial justification, facades/interiors/finishes, daylight/insolation, acoustic protection, room programme and relevant drawings with conditional items; this is a routing summary, not the complete content list. Retrieve the current resolution and the relevant section before claiming a submission complete.

## gost-r-21-101-2026

- **Identity:** ГОСТ Р 21.101-2026, «Система проектной документации для строительства. Основные требования к проектной и рабочей документации»; national standard, Росстандарт.

- **Scope:** General project/working-documentation requirements for objects of various purposes through construction, reconstruction, capital repair, operation and liquidation. Survey reports are included only for the stated general text/graphic rules in section 5 and change rules in section 7. These section numbers are reported by the official scope card; their detailed requirements were not read.
- **Edition/status:** Card says «Действует», order 12.02.2026 No. 129-ст, commencement 01.04.2026, replaces ГОСТ Р 21.101-2020. It lists [correction ИУС 11-2026](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/changesdetails/c0ff9ff2-26b1-48d1-8b3b-0d2a0df11f23); correction content could not be retrieved. The document viewer returned protected/empty content.
- **Application basis:** Documentation standard selected through the applicable law/register/assignment/contract route; current status alone does not make all its clauses universally mandatory. Check its actual provisions and registry/transition basis where relevant.
- **Related:** `government-87-project-documentation`; `gost-21-501-2018`; predecessor 21.101-2020 is historical and must not remain an unqualified current default.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; official scope/status metadata verified, body and correction content partial/unavailable. Obtain actual applicable clauses and correction before issuing a documentation compliance conclusion.

## gost-21-501-2018

- **Identity:** ГОСТ 21.501-2018, «Система проектной документации для строительства. Правила выполнения рабочей документации архитектурных и конструктивных решений»; interstate standard, adopted by МГС and introduced in Russia by Росстандарт.
- **Authority:** [Official Rosstandart card](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/a241e289-aac3-4ee2-ad44-3259f9774799): «Область применения», registration, MGS and replacement fields read.
- **Scope:** Composition and presentation of architectural/structural working documentation for buildings of various purposes and construction products. The card routes metal structural documentation to ГОСТ 21.502 and timber to ГОСТ 21.504; their scope/body have not been reviewed here.
- **Edition/status:** Card says «Действует», Росстандарт order 18.12.2018 No. 1121-ст, commencement 01.06.2019, MGS protocol 111-П dated 30.08.2018; replaces ГОСТ 21.501-2011. No replacement shown. No correction documents returned by the card; this is not a claim that all amendments have independently been audited.
- **Application basis:** Conditional documentation route under the application mechanism above. Its working-documentation scope does not define all project-stage or concept deliverables.
- **Related:** `gost-r-21-101-2026`; `government-87-project-documentation`; metal/timber drawing standards are additional discovery routes when those packages are required.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01; official scope/status metadata verified; protected viewer yielded no usable body. Read actual drawing/schedule clauses before applying them; no sheet format or modeling workflow is imposed by this catalogue.
