# Building typologies: Russian normative source routes

Use the object, individual spaces, work stage, and relevant edition to select source documents. A register's statement that a document is in force is not a determination that every provision is mandatory for a project. Resolve application basis and transition dates through [federal framework](federal-framework.md) and the project record; assess fire, sanitary and accessibility rules through their separate thematic routes. These are Russian federal sources; local conditions, special objects and applicable territorial requirements still require review.

## Coverage

Registry scope abstracts and status reviewed for housing, public and industrial buildings, schools, preschool, medical, parking, selected sports, theatre, shopping complexes and hotels below. The primary register supports the catalog claims identified in each record. Check the applicable text, changes and project dates when selecting provisions. The [official SP register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp) is the discovery route; check its individual cards and changes again for project work.

Discovery-only gaps: standalone retail outside shopping complexes, sports uses outside СП 332, other cultural institutions, boarding residence buildings and specialty warehouses excluded by СП 56 need individual source discovery. For an area decision, define its purpose and retrieve the selected applicable definition from the primary СП 54, СП 118 or СП 160 source and relevant legal source. Registry metadata does not establish area inclusions, exclusions or a universal formula. No exhaustive subtype coverage or detailed measurement check is claimed. Screen host and embedded functions separately and follow the affected sanitary, fire and accessibility routes.

## sp-54-13330-2022

- **Identity:** СП 54.13330.2022 «Здания жилые многоквартирные», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/c8b00dd7-7c13-4f17-ac0c-d9cf70311263), sections «Основные сведения», «Область применения», «Ссылочные данные» and «Изменения и поправки».
- **Scope:** The primary registry scope covers design of new and reconstructed multiunit residential buildings (including the stated block-building and apartment-type dormitory cases), and residential spaces in buildings of other uses, within its stated building-height limit of 75 m. The height definition and detailed exclusions require the actual provision when relevant. Mixed uses need their affected public, sanitary, fire and accessibility routes; the abstract does not establish all project exceptions.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 14 June 2022.
- **Application basis:** Select provisions according to the project and [federal framework](federal-framework.md); registry currency alone does not make the whole SP mandatory.
- **Related:** СП 55 for single-family houses; СП 118 for public spaces; separate fire, sanitary and accessibility routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: primary registry identity, issuer, scope abstract, status read. Detailed scope/height definitions, area provisions and affecting amendments are checked when selecting them for a project.

## sp-55-13330-2016

- **Identity:** СП 55.13330.2016 «Дома жилые одноквартирные», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/a99146d7-4dc6-4bb8-96e0-35e0233050c2), «Основные сведения», «Область применения», «Ссылочные данные».
- **Scope:** Design, construction and reconstruction of detached or block-development single-family houses within the registry's floor-count limit, including houses with integrated or attached public or craft/agricultural production uses. Classify both the dwelling and subsidiary uses; check the exact scope conditions in the text.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 21 April 2017.
- **Application basis:** Project-specific selection; see [federal framework](federal-framework.md) rather than treating the entire code as mandatory.
- **Related:** СП 54 for multiunit residential, СП 118 for public functions, СП 56 for industrial functions, СП 113 for parking.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: official register scope abstract and status read. Check exclusions and current text for the project.

## sp-118-13330-2022

- **Identity:** СП 118.13330.2022 «Общественные здания и сооружения», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт primary register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/ced01945-2f53-47c9-bdb8-4875c2b9800e), identity/issuer, «Область применения» and status.
- **Scope:** The primary registry scope covers design of public buildings and facilities in new construction, reconstruction and capital repair, including functional-use changes; also public-use premises embedded in housing and other objects under the stated sanitary conditions. Classify host and public rooms separately. The card does not establish detailed area definitions, all exclusions or project-specific requirements.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 20 June 2022.
- **Application basis:** Use [federal framework](federal-framework.md) to determine the basis of particular provisions; registration does not itself impose all provisions.
- **Related:** Use alongside the relevant specialized education, healthcare, sports, cultural, retail or hospitality code; separate sanitary, accessibility and fire routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: primary registry identity, scope, status read. Retrieve the relevant primary text and affecting changes when a measurement decision requires them.

## sp-56-13330-2021

- **Identity:** СП 56.13330.2021 «Производственные здания», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/e3c56fe9-8768-4597-b5d1-92c72c6f3d5a), scope and reference data.
- **Scope:** Design of production and laboratory buildings and rooms, workshops and warehouses, including spaces built into buildings of other functional classes. The register excludes explosives and military premises, underground metro and mining works, and many specialized storage goods and facilities including refrigerators and grain stores. Consult its complete exclusion list before applying; a warehouse label alone is insufficient.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 28 January 2022. Its amendment area returned no documents; this does not establish that no changes exist.
- **Application basis:** Particular provisions depend on project and [federal framework](federal-framework.md); do not infer universal mandatory force from registry status.
- **Related:** СП 44 for associated administrative and welfare spaces; fire and sanitary routes for respective issues.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: official registry scope including exclusion list, status read; full text not checked. Verify commodity-specific rules, current text and project dates.

## sp-44-13330-2011

- **Identity:** СП 44.13330.2011 «Административные и бытовые здания», code of practice, Минрегион России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/d4e8fda2-e2a9-45a4-b686-d21347d36ab2), registry «Область применения», status and reference data.
- **Scope:** Administrative and welfare buildings serving industrial enterprises that are new, expanded, reconstructed or technically re-equipped, within the height boundary in the registry. It excludes public buildings and facilities and, except for specified provisions, mobile buildings. Do not classify every office as an industrial administration building.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 20 May 2011.
- **Application basis:** Particular selected provisions and their legal basis require the [federal framework](federal-framework.md) route.
- **Related:** СП 56 industrial buildings; СП 118 public buildings.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: official register scope, exclusions and status read. Confirm current text and project transition.

## sp-160-1325800-2014

- **Identity:** СП 160.1325800.2014 «Здания и комплексы многофункциональные. Правила проектирования», code of practice, Минстрой России.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт primary register](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/c71e37b2-43b8-4c82-8de2-1c6cf2826896), identity/issuer, scope and status.
- **Scope:** The primary registry scope covers design/construction of new, reconstructed and capital-repaired mixed-use buildings, complexes and their sites. It states building height up to 75 m, public floors no higher than 55 m and underground depth up to 15 m, and excludes design of seasonal/mobile mixed-use buildings and structures. Check the actual definitions and selected provisions when those limits matter. A mixed-use scope does not make this a general standalone retail code.
- **Edition/status:** Register marks in force from 1 September 2014.
- **Application basis:** Select provisions under the applicable project basis via [federal framework](federal-framework.md); registry status does not make the whole document universally mandatory.
- **Related:** СП 54 for residential components, СП 118 for public components, СП 113 for parking and relevant specialized-use branches. Define the measurement purpose and obtain its primary area provisions before comparing measures; no appendix-derived formula is imported here.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: primary register identity, scope boundaries/exclusion and status read. Detailed measurement provisions and affecting changes remain project checks when needed.
