## Select Russian sources

When Russia is confirmed, select the matching question/condition from
[task-to-record routes](routes.md), then use its specific record links.
The [catalog](index.md) owns coverage and discovery-source limits.
The catalog's Russian adaptation target does
not assign Russia to every future task. Do not read every record for a local
decision.

1. Establish the actual subject: address/municipality and parcel, host and
   embedded uses, users, work classification, height/depth/scale, existing
   conditions, design stage and relevant project dates. Distinguish premises
   alteration, reconstruction and capital repair; a user's word “renovation”
   alone does not settle the legal classification. Retain fixed project facts
   and identify only missing conditions material to selection.
2. Select the relevant [work/stage](routes.md#work-and-timing) record;
   then screen territory, typology, fire/accessibility, sanitary/environmental
   and engineering-interface branches against the actual task. Include
   adjacent documents where a changed entrance, use, envelope or route affects
   them. Check both the host building and an embedded function.
3. Read each candidate record completely using [selective reading](../../requirements.md#complete-selective-reading), including its scope, exclusions and verification limits. Check normative links under [primary sources](../../requirements.md#primary-normative-sources).
   Follow the linked official text/register to the relevant provision and
   current scope/status evidence necessary for the decision. A registry
   abstract supports discovery, not every detailed rule. An active badge,
   search excerpt or agency-hosted old commercial text cannot establish a
   current provision. Keep inconsistent status/replacement fields unresolved.
4. Establish the application basis for the selected provision and project
   date: direct legal/sanitary/fire obligation, a documented technical safety
   route, or a project/contract requirement. The construction requirements
   register and fire-safety mechanisms need separate checks in the
   [federal record](federal-framework.md#coverage-and-application-mechanism). Do not classify a whole SP/GOST as
   universally mandatory or voluntary from its title, registration or an old
   list. Do not infer that a missing registry search result removes a statutory
   duty. An alternative substantiation route needs an actual justified project
   decision, not a shortcut around a constraint.
5. Select the edition for the actual work/stage/date and scope of intervention.
   Compare any transition provisions, approved baseline and establishing acts.
   For a date-dependent edition or transition question, inspect the relevant
   external primary source and retain the project conclusion with its owner.
   Do not maintain a revision history in this shared navigator.
6. Assign a [shared selection outcome](../../requirements.md#selection-outcomes) and identify the source fragment, factual
   trigger, edition/date condition and remaining question. Store the result in
   the existing project's requirements/source record, with its design response
   and owner. Keep shared discovery metadata in the catalog; do not create a
   parallel project-fact store.

## Apply the route to a bounded change

For a relocated entrance in an existing apartment building, first retain the
accepted layout and identify whether the change affects common property,
load-bearing elements, façade, access, evacuation or an embedded public use.
Check the housing/work-classification route and local/heritage conditions;
screen residential/public typology and fire/accessibility together. If the
municipality, scope of intervention or approvals are unknown, select potential
sources and leave their affected provisions unresolved. Do not silently treat
the task as either new construction or harmless interior decoration.

## Choose the area and capacity basis

Ask what the figure decides: furniture fit, a floor program, a dwelling total,
a public-building total, cadastral reporting or a population-dependent check.
Retain geometric measurements and their bounding faces separately from formal
Russian categories. Select [use and area](routes.md#use-and-area) records for
the actual host and embedded activity; obtain the relevant current provision
before naming its inclusions/exclusions. Общая, полезная and расчетная площадь
are purpose-dependent categories, not translations of GFA/GIA/NIA with unchanged
totals. Apartment housing-law, design and cadastral measurements also require
their own selected basis; the catalog is not a complete cadastral method.

Keep room, wall, core, circulation and exterior components visible so that a
changed definition can be recomputed instead of multiplied by a presumed
efficiency factor. Use [area accounting](../../program-and-space.md#how-can-areas-be-counted-once-and-compared)
for the finished-face correction and [comparison](../../computational-design.md#frame-the-question)
for its option-ranking consequence. Neither geometric result verifies a formal
Russian area rule. Likewise, retain clear room height, floor spacing and the
actual height datums separately; a geometric roof height does not establish a
typology or fire-height definition.

Separate seats, staff, overlapping activities and queues by time and location.
Their operating peak informs program and fit; a prescribed population for
evacuation or sanitary facilities needs its own provision and actual use.
Do not let an unresolved formal metric receive a passing compliance score.

## Classify use and works before applying a rule

Describe what takes place, for whom, when and with what equipment/process,
including the host, embedded uses and affected neighbors. A public consultation
room is not screened out because its host is residential; a community workshop
is not classified as industrial from its name. The checked
[СП 118 scope](building-typologies.md#sp-118-13330-2022) includes embedded public
premises under its stated conditions. Select specialized typology/sanitary
branches only when the actual activity triggers them.

For existing objects, list the physical delta: room/premises boundaries,
partitions, services/equipment, bearing elements, facade openings, common routes
and land. Select [work and timing](routes.md#work-and-timing), the
[ГрК](federal-framework.md#grk-190-design-and-expertise) and, where premises in an
apartment building are affected, [ЖК](federal-framework.md#zhk-188-alterations)
questions independently. An unchanged envelope does not establish a legal
exemption; neither “renovation” nor a furniture change alone determines all
legal work categories. Read current operative definitions/procedure and the
project's property facts before assigning approval, consent or exemption.
Use the [changed-entrance correction](../../design-development.md#turn-critique-into-a-spatial-correction)
to preserve a functioning base plan while the distinct legal decision is open.

## Resolve entrances, routes and mixed uses

Overlay resident/public/staff/goods/waste journeys and opening hours on one
plan, including destinations above/below arrival and routes through common
property. Reserve actual landing, door, waiting and vertical-connection space
before freezing the core. Compare keeping an upper public destination with
moving it to arrival level; recount the displaced use and section consequences.
The [mixed-use method and worked correction](../../planning-and-section.md#resolve-a-russian-mixed-use-plan-before-fixing-the-core)
develop these alternatives without declaring a prescribed entrance solution.

Select [safety and access](routes.md#safety-and-access) for the affected use and
works. Keep ordinary inclusive access and evacuation as separate questions;
the same drawn door/lift arrow cannot verify both. Distinguish program use,
functional fire-hazard classification and process/storage category. Obtain
actual process/materials and the relevant classification provisions before
fixing fire separation, connections, exit count or dimensions. The СП 59 scope
trigger does not verify a route width, ramp slope, lift strategy or permission
to share an exit. Room lighting, insolation, noise and sanitary questions also
remain separate; use [room-condition alternatives](../../performance-and-coordination.md#fit-russian-room-conditions-without-merging-their-tests)
to change facade/use allocation while their particular targets are selected.

## Select climate, site and assembly inputs

Establish actual locality, parcel, intended season, indoor humidity and heating/
cooling schedule. Select [environment and interfaces](routes.md#environment-and-interfaces)
and parcel-specific [local discovery](territory-and-special-conditions.md#local-discovery-routes).
A city climate observation supplies neither a parcel restriction nor a design
load. Read current СП 131 parameters and their project-date basis before
technical use; coordinate structural, thermal, moisture and system calculations
with their specialist owners. Architecture still owns the changed lobby, shade,
roof/water path, service depth, junction intent and maintenance access.

The [two-location study](../../composition-and-style.md#two-russian-reading-pavilions-climate-and-use-change-the-edge)
uses primary Hydrometcentre observations for [Yakutsk](https://meteoinfo.ru/categ-articles/15-climate-cat/klimaticheskie-normy/clim-towns/1782-1246618396)
and [Krasnodar](https://meteoinfo.ru/categ-articles/15-climate-cat/klimaticheskie-normy/clim-towns/1699-1246618396),
monthly tables and their **1961–1990** averaging-period field, checked
2026-11-02. They support a descriptive seasonal contrast only. The different
arrival/outdoor-use priorities are explicit hypothetical briefs; neither city
name nor those historical averages prove current design temperatures, loads,
glare, comfort or a sufficient assembly.

Check exclusions as carefully as triggers. A separate public building heated
only two days per week falls within the checked СП 50 periodic-heating
exclusion for that document's selection; an occasionally heated room in a
daily heated host is a different scope question. Reopen selection when use or
schedule changes. An existing finish-only revision with no ground/foundation/
load intervention supplies no trigger for foundation design for that bounded
decision. Neither exclusion waives other requirements. Develop the affected
[assembly and spatial reservations](../../envelope-and-assemblies.md#adapt-the-family-to-climate-use-and-works)
and record the missing alternative technical basis rather than inventing one.

## Evidence limits for these adaptations

| Owning record and fragment actually read | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [СП 118](building-typologies.md#sp-118-13330-2022), primary card “Область применения”, checked 2026-11-02 | Host/embedded public-use screening | Current area/height definitions or a particular required entrance |
| [СП 59](fire-and-accessibility.md#sp-59-13330-2020), primary card scope and private single-family exclusion, checked 2026-11-02 | Public-access source-selection question | Operative entrance, level-connection, evacuation or dimension requirements |
| [СП 50](environment-and-interfaces.md#sp-50-13330-2024), primary card complete scope/exclusions, checked 2026-11-02 | Conditional selection/exclusion for a building's actual use and heating schedule | Thermal design inputs, a sufficient assembly or an exemption from other requirements |

Use the four canonical outcomes: **Applicable**, **Potential**, **Not applicable**,
**Unresolved**. A plausible scope with missing project facts is Potential;
inaccessible current text, uncertain basis or conflicting evidence leaves the
affected provision Unresolved. “With conditions” describes the recorded facts,
not a fifth outcome or a verified pending provision. Scope evidence alone does
not close detailed design checks. Where operative primary text is unavailable,
retain the exact missing definition/provision and useful provisional design;
do not promote a search excerpt, unestablished distributor or old base edition
to a verified current rule.
