## coverage-and-application-mechanism

Coverage is federal design/expertise procedure, building-safety justification, housing alteration/consent, project-documentation composition and documentation standards. Select the document for the decision in the contents above, then use its scope and evidence limits. Fire/accessibility, sanitary, heritage and environmental sources have separate topic owners. Laws 184-ФЗ/162-ФЗ and energy-saving law 261-ФЗ are additional routes when their mechanisms are relevant, not reviewed records here.

| Evidence available | Records | Selection limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official scope/status metadata read | ГОСТ Р 21.101-2026; ГОСТ 21.501-2018 | Scope supports selection; detailed provisions were not read. |
| Operator explanation and provision-level register evidence | Government 1417 route | Requirement ID 152316 content/version/status read; ID 152314 indexed-only. Neither verifies the Government rules. |
| Instrument and particular primary addresses identified; body unavailable | GrK 190-ФЗ; ЖК 188-ФЗ; 169-ФЗ; 384-ФЗ; 653-ФЗ; Government 87 | Subjects are identified; current legal scope, exclusions and status remain unverified. |

Links lead to official text banks, issuer publications or primary registers. An operator explanation supports its own statements, not the underlying law. An active standard card or register entry does not make a whole SP/GOST mandatory or establish project applicability. Check only the operative provisions, exceptions and dates needed for the decision using the [applicability method](applicability.md); record resulting project requirements in [requirements](../../requirements.md).

## grk-190-design-and-expertise

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 190-ФЗ, «Градостроительный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Pravo text-bank record 102090643](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090643) and [Presidential act-bank record 21916](http://www.kremlin.ru/acts/bank/21916) were found and identified as primary addresses for this Code. Their bodies were inaccessible at the source check; operative legal text was not read.
- **Scope:** Route for classifying the object/work and selecting design, documentation and expertise procedures: article 1 classifications, 48 design, 49 expertise and 57.4 register. Current work/stage conditions and exclusions remain primary-unverified; no exemption for individual housing, repair or small objects is established here.
- **Edition/status:** Current operative edition/status and decision-specific effective dates remain unanswered by the inaccessible bodies. Access failure is not evidence that the Code is missing or repealed.
- **Application basis:** Federal procedural-law route. Confirm the relevant classification/procedure and its exceptions before relying on it; register inclusion alone does not determine project applicability.
- **Related:** [government-87-project-documentation](#government-87-project-documentation); [fz-384-building-safety](#fz-384-building-safety); [fz-653-building-safety-reform](#fz-653-building-safety-reform); [government-1417-requirements-register](#government-1417-requirements-register); territorial planning/GPZU/PZZ routes.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: primary addresses and identity identified; operative scope, exceptions and current status not read. Use a targeted primary provision check when the selected procedure or exemption matters.

## zhk-188-alterations

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 29.12.2004 No. 188-ФЗ, «Жилищный кодекс Российской Федерации»; federal code, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Official code text-bank record](https://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102090645), identified but body inaccessible at the source check.
- **Scope:** Russian housing-code candidate for alterations/replanning of premises in apartment buildings and common-property interfaces. Reading routes: articles 25–29 for classification/approval/completion, 36/40 for common property. The exact current boundary between works, exclusions, consent triggers and completion conditions is not primary-verified here. Do not infer consent or exemption from a proposed room layout.
- **Edition/status:** Current consolidated edition/status was not read in primary text.
- **Application basis:** Federal housing procedure and property/consent rights within its apartment-building scope. A room-layout drawing does not itself authorize works. Coordinate municipal procedure, building safety, heritage and registration requirements; separate reconstruction from housing replanning on actual facts.
- **Related:** [grk-190-design-and-expertise](#grk-190-design-and-expertise); [fz-169-architectural-activity](#fz-169-architectural-activity); residential-building and heritage records. Check whether the relevant provision includes nonresidential premises inside an apartment building; other object types need their own scope check.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: particular primary code address identified; operative scope/status unavailable. An alteration/consent decision needs the relevant current provision and municipal procedure.

- **Authority:** [Official text-bank record](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102038230), timed out. [Mintrud professional-knowledge guide](https://mintrud.gov.ru/guide/default/index?page=5), category П.17.2, lists this law's identity. The guide is a primary publication of Mintrud's own knowledge requirements, not primary law text or evidence of this law's current scope/status.
- **Scope:** Discovery-only candidate for professional architectural activity, architectural-project/object changes and relations with architecture authorities. Read current articles 1–3, 12–15, 20–22 before relying on specific duties, author consent or approval relationships; no current scope clause was verified in primary text here.
- **Edition/status:** Current operative edition/status was not verified in primary law text; the professional-knowledge guide does not establish it.
- **Application basis:** Potential federal legal/contractual duties, subject to current text and actual scope. It does not substitute for GrK/local architectural appearance, heritage or permit checks.
- **Related:** [grk-190-design-and-expertise](#grk-190-design-and-expertise); documentation/review operations; heritage and intellectual-property interfaces where triggered.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: law identified in the primary agency guide; legal-bank body inaccessible. Professional-duty or author-consent conclusions require the relevant current primary provisions.

## fz-384-building-safety

- **Identity:** Federal Law of 30.12.2009 No. 384-ФЗ, «Технический регламент о безопасности зданий и сооружений»; federal technical regulation, Russian Federation.
- **Authority:** [Official text-bank record for 384-ФЗ](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&nd=102135277), identified but body inaccessible. [FAU FCC register/justification explanation](https://faufcc.ru/deiatelnost/stu) was read as the operator’s own explanation, not as law text.
- **Scope:** Building/structure safety and justification of architectural decisions. Article 3 is the scope/exclusions route; articles 6/15 are the justification route. Detailed lifecycle reach, technological-process exclusion and specialized-object conditions remain primary-unverified.
- **Edition/status:** Current technical-regulation edition/status was not read in primary act text; the operator explanation does not resolve legal commencement or transition conditions.
- **Application basis:** Potential federal technical-regulation requirements, distinguished from a selected standard or other means of justification. The operator notice describes several supporting-document/method routes; use the act's relevant operative provision for a later normative conclusion.
- **Related:** GrK 190-ФЗ; fire law 123-ФЗ; accessibility, sanitary and performance topic records. These interfaces do not authorize replacing their separate legal checks.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: particular primary act address identified, body inaccessible; operator explanation read in its own role. Confirm the relevant safety/justification provision and exclusions for the decision.

## government-1417-requirements-register

- **Identity:** Government of the Russian Federation Resolution of 31.08.2023 No. 1417; publication title «Об утверждении Правил формирования и ведения реестра требований, подлежащих применению при проведении экспертизы проектной документации и (или) экспертизы результатов инженерных изысканий, осуществлении архитектурно-строительного проектирования, строительства, реконструкции, капитального ремонта, эксплуатации и сноса объектов капитального строительства, и о признании утратившим силу постановления Правительства Российской Федерации от 12 сентября 2020 г. № 1417»; Government register rules.
- **Authority:** [Official publication](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202308310014) and [official PDF](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/file/pdf?eoNumber=0001202308310014) yielded headings, not operative rules. [FAU FCC explanation](https://faufcc.ru/deiatelnost/stu) was read as operator content. [Primary-register page 269993](https://xn--e1ahdbdflckekkc.xn--p1ai/rntd/paragraph/269993), requirement ID 152316/clause 5.21, yielded actual content and metadata on 2026-11-01. [Parent 6351](https://xn--e1ahdbdflckekkc.xn--p1ai/rntd/6351) and [page 270150](https://xn--e1ahdbdflckekkc.xn--p1ai/rntd/paragraph/270150), ID 152314/clause 5.19, remain indexed-only evidence.
- **Scope:** Requirements-register procedure; current rules’ operative scope/exclusions were not read. Live ID 152316 concerns precipitation protection over entrance platforms, exterior stair flights and facade-side ramps, snow/drainage coordination and a projecting-floor/facade alternative, referencing SP 20.13330 and clause 6.2.1.16. This supports that entry’s content, not its applicability to every project; clause 5.19 passage-opening content remains indexed-only.
- **Edition/status:** ID 152316 displayed «Действует», «Текущее требование», version 1, registry publication 09.04.2025 and parent СП 54.13330.2022. Publication in the register is not commencement of the underlying document. Current consolidated Government rules/status remain unverified.
- **Application basis:** Procedural register under GrK/384-ФЗ. Inspect the selected requirement and project conditions; inclusion does not establish whole-document mandatory status.
- **Related:** GrK article 57.4; [384-ФЗ](#fz-384-building-safety); [transition route](#fz-653-building-safety-reform). Government 815 is a date-dependent additional route only if the project’s transition actually calls for it.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: ID 152316 actual primary-register text and displayed version/status read; ID 152314 indexed-only. Rules’ operative scope/status remains a separate primary-text gap.

- **Authority:** [Government act publication](http://government.ru/docs/all/63014/) and [official text-bank record](http://pravo.gov.ru/proxy/ips/?docbody=&link_id=0&nd=102119991&firstDoc=1), identified as Resolution 87 but bodies inaccessible at the source check.
- **Scope:** Project-documentation section composition/content. Paragraphs 1–4 are the scope/composition route and 13 the architectural-content route. Object/use, construction/reconstruction/capital-repair, linear-object and stage/expertise conditions remain primary-unverified.
- **Edition/status:** Current operative edition/status and effective conditions were not verified in primary text.
- **Application basis:** Government project-documentation regulation candidate, within its actual scope once verified. Do not impose its section set on an early concept from the title alone; inspect the relevant scope/content provision when deliverables are being decided.
- **Related:** GrK articles 48–49; [gost-r-21-101-2026](#gost-r-21-101-2026); [gost-21-501-2018](#gost-21-501-2018); documentation operation. The standards address documentation rules, while this resolution controls applicable project-documentation composition.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: primary issuer/text-bank addresses identified; bodies unavailable. Confirm the relevant scope/content provision when deciding project-documentation deliverables.

## gost-r-21-101-2026

- **Identity:** ГОСТ Р 21.101-2026, «Система проектной документации для строительства. Основные требования к проектной и рабочей документации»; national standard, Росстандарт.
- **Authority:** [Official Rosstandart card](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/17bc12e8-6579-4145-b141-56855e772e7f): «Область применения», «Регистрация в фонде», «Ссылочные данные» and correction list read.
- **Scope:** General project/working-documentation requirements for objects of various purposes through construction, reconstruction, capital repair, operation and liquidation. Survey reports are included only for the stated general text/graphic rules in section 5 and change rules in section 7. These section numbers are reported by the official scope card; their detailed requirements were not read.
- **Edition/status:** Card says «Действует», Росстандарт order 12.02.2026 No. 129-ст, commencement 01.04.2026. [Correction ИУС 11-2026](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/changesdetails/c0ff9ff2-26b1-48d1-8b3b-0d2a0df11f23) is listed; its content was inaccessible. Select this current card rather than defaulting to ГОСТ Р 21.101-2020.
- **Application basis:** Documentation standard selected through the applicable law/register/assignment/contract route; current status alone does not make all its clauses universally mandatory. Check its actual provisions and registry/transition basis where relevant.
- **Related:** [Government 87](#government-87-project-documentation); [ГОСТ 21.501-2018](#gost-21-501-2018).
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: official scope/status metadata read; protected viewer yielded no usable body and correction content was unavailable. Read the provisions and correction relevant to the documentation decision.
