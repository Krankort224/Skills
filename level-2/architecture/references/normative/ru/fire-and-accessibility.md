## Coverage and evidence

The records below distinguish registry scope text from full normative text. The Russian Federation is the default territorial context; special facilities, existing buildings, changed uses, work stages and special regimes require individual scope checks. Registry entries and official publication pages are evidence of what they actually state, not proof of a complete consolidated edition. Official registry scope text has been read for SP 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12, 59, 136, 456 and 132 and ГОСТ Р 59602. Further branches and unverified sources are explicitly marked below; a discovery entry is a lead, not an assertion of applicability.

## sp-1-13130-2020

- **Identity:** СП 1.13130.2020 «Системы противопожарной защиты. Эвакуационные пути и выходы»; свод правил, МЧС России, order 19.03.2020 № 194.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт record, registration and «Область применения»](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/9fdcb635-708c-4c67-81a7-83e6baad9ab3); [Росстандарт 123-ФЗ list, item 111](https://protect.gost.ru/technicalregulations3.aspx?RegNum=56). For the selected seating provision, [VNIIPO's official MChS act catalogue](https://vniipo.ru/normativno-pravovoe-regulirovanie-v-oblasti-pb/normativnye-pravovye-akty-mchs-rossii/) explicitly publishes [Order 16.06.2025 No. 502 and amendment 3](https://vniipo.ru/ufiles/ufiles/NPAPB/502_16.06.2025.pdf), PDF pp. 1–2, complete replacement §7.3.3 on p. 2. Publisher role is the SP developer's own institutional act publication: the Rosstandart record names ФГБУ ВНИИПО МЧС России as developer, and its catalogue links this exact order; this is not authority inferred merely from a hostname.
- **Scope:** Registry scope covers evacuation and emergency routes and exits, including outdoor technological installations; design, functional-use change and reconstruction, capital repair or technical re-equipment within the work's extent. It excludes specified special-purpose facilities, certain tall buildings and multiple-basement cases subject to stated exceptions; read the full wording and height definitions rather than treating this as a universal rule. Assess new use and altered layouts under the scope text. Accessibility of evacuation needs joint assessment with SP 59 and this SP's relevant provisions.
- **Edition/status:** Registry says действующий, introduced 19.09.2020, and lists amendments 1–3. [Amendment 3 registration](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/changesdetails/de574ad5-67fc-4e31-a0a5-a793c94b1cc7) identifies Order 502 and publication/effectivity 03.07.2025, consistent with the order's publication-based commencement. Its complete replacement §7.3.3 is the selected current fragment checked 2026-11-02; earlier wording need not be reconstructed for that replacement. Other consolidated provisions and project-date exceptions were not read.
- **Application basis:** Selected fire-safety provisions and regulatory-compliance routes require provision-specific verification; see [federal-framework.md](federal-framework.md), not a blanket mandate. The [seating example](applicability.md#apply-a-verified-seating-provision) explicitly adopts §7.3.3 in its hypothetical project brief. This establishes a project requirement for that decision, not a verified statutory route for every object.
- **Related:** 123-ФЗ; SP 2, 4, 59; high-rise and deep-basement special routes where scope excludes the object.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-02: the SP and amendment-3 cards, exact institutional catalogue entry and two-page Order 502 publication were read, extracted and visually inspected, including full §7.3.3. Registry scope/registration and list item 111 carried from 2026-11-01. That clause covers auditorium, assembly and conference-hall seating, with its stated balcony/box exception; floor fixing is the normal arrangement, and omission for a hall with at most 200 seats requires interconnection in rows. This verifies only the selected seating provision, not aisle dimensions, exit capacity, connector performance, full §1/§7.3 context or the statutory application route.
