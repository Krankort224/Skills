## Coverage and evidence

The records below distinguish registry scope text from full normative text. The Russian Federation is the default territorial context; special facilities, existing buildings, changed uses, work stages and special regimes require individual scope checks. Registry entries and official publication pages are evidence of what they actually state, not proof of a complete consolidated edition. Official registry scope text has been read for SP 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12, 59 and 136 and ГОСТ Р 59602. Further branches and unverified sources are explicitly marked below; a discovery entry is a lead, not an assertion of applicability.

## sp-1-13130-2020

- **Identity:** СП 1.13130.2020 «Системы противопожарной защиты. Эвакуационные пути и выходы»; свод правил, МЧС России, order 19.03.2020 № 194.
- **Authority:** [Росстандарт record, registration and «Область применения»](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/9fdcb635-708c-4c67-81a7-83e6baad9ab3); [Росстандарт 123-ФЗ list, item 111](https://protect.gost.ru/technicalregulations3.aspx?RegNum=56).
- **Scope:** Registry scope covers evacuation and emergency routes and exits, including outdoor technological installations; design, functional-use change and reconstruction, capital repair or technical re-equipment within the work's extent. It excludes specified special-purpose facilities, certain tall buildings and multiple-basement cases subject to stated exceptions; read the full wording and height definitions rather than treating this as a universal rule. Assess new use and altered layouts under the scope text. Accessibility of evacuation needs joint assessment with SP 59 and this SP's relevant provisions.
- **Edition/status:** Registry says действующий, introduced 19.09.2020. The linked list records changes 1–3 from 03.07.2025 until further change; this does not establish exhaustive consolidation on the project date.
- **Application basis:** Selected fire-safety provisions and regulatory-compliance routes require provision-specific verification; see [federal-framework.md](federal-framework.md), not a blanket mandate.
- **Related:** 123-ФЗ; SP 2, 4, 59; high-rise and deep-basement special routes where scope excludes the object.
- **Verification:** Checked 2026-11-01: official registry scope and registration, and list item 111 read; full SP text, exceptions in their current consolidated form and project-specific application remain to be checked.

## Special-object discovery and unresolved interfaces

- **High-rise:** [SP 267.1325800.2016 registry scope](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/e343722d-53fe-4dd1-955b-2e1946442f50) covers new high-rise building and complex design and construction, excludes capital repair, reconstruction, modernization and high-rise structures, and describes a conditional public/multifunctional use. Its change № 3 [record](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/changesdetails/f62e4140-c0e1-4685-8f54-d05ddad6314f) leaves the effective-date field blank. [SP 477.1325800.2020 registry](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/98f1ad5e-f762-413c-ac5c-3c5c8d36071d) says cancelled even though its historical scope covers certain high-rise fire-safety cases; identify the actual current successor and transition. Do not infer a gap in regulation from cancellation. Route the general building record to its typological owner.
- **Multifunctional:** SP 456 above provides the fire-specific route; verify scope, boundaries and edition. [SP 160.1325800.2014](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/c71e37b2-43b8-4c82-8de2-1c6cf2826896) is a separate general building design route with explicit geometry and stage boundaries; use its full scope and coordinate with the typological owner.
- **Parking:** [SP 506.1311500.2021 registry](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/details/eebc4773-8dd3-4196-a246-02c287feb6f4) says cancelled, and its [official change record](https://protect.gost.ru/sp/changesdetails/42da8dbe-5f80-4853-8268-0f82a0eaf045) identifies cancellation of its approval from 10.10.2023. The [123-ФЗ list](https://protect.gost.ru/technicalregulations3.aspx?RegNum=56) identifies SP 113.13330.2023 and SP 154.13130.2013 as leads. Current precise scope, relationship to SP 4 and project-date applicability are unresolved; consult typological records and current official texts.
- **Other interfaces:** Signalling, automatic detection/extinguishing, fire-service access, fire signs and special-use or special-purpose facilities require further source review; they are not covered comprehensively here. No full SP was read. Every record above is partial, not a verified current consolidated text; discovery entries are not records of applicability.
