# Program: demand, resources and allocation

Choose the activity question first. Demand and adjacency intent do not establish physical room fit; source benchmarks are not project requirements. Numerical examples are hypothetical.

Open a known method directly. Otherwise choose one question below and follow only the dependencies relevant to the decision; reading every sibling or the source ledger is unnecessary.

| Question | Local method and distinction |
| --- | --- |
| What must be accommodated? | [demand](demand.md) — Identify inputs, status, concurrency and conflicts. |
| Can rooms, staff and equipment actually be shared? | [shared use](shared-use.md) — Test occupancy, setup, changeover and displaced objects. |
| What supporting functions are missing? | [support program](support-program.md) — Derive storage, staff and other support from actual use. |
| What capacity and room envelope follow? | [capacity and room demand](capacity-and-room-demand.md) — Keep activity demand, object/action space and normative population distinct. |
| What area is being counted? | [area ledger](area-ledger.md) — Set boundaries, reconcile totals and connect the ledger to geometry. |
| Which relationships should organize the plan? | [relationships](relationships.md) — Turn adjacency, separation and shared support into alternatives. |
| How should uses occupy floors and change over time? | [floors and change](floors-and-change.md) — Preserve level, phasing and future-state consequences. |
| Can independent hours and support actually work? | [access and closure](../space/circulation/access-and-closure.md) — Trace every portal and complete visitor/service journey. |
| How should a permitted local change be judged? | [bounded revision](../methods/bounded-revision.md) — Preserve the accepted baseline and propagate affected consequences. |

For questions outside this migrated group use the existing [coverage map](../knowledge-map.md). Its nonpilot owners remain at their current paths until the remaining reviewed migration.
