# Recount housing yield across different floors

This authored hypothetical allocation tests the return from
[preliminary area/massing](../../references/program/area-to-massing.md) to
actual section/floor occurrences. It is a count and area study, not furnished
apartments, fitted cores or a completed building. Use the existing
[section-variant method](../../references/typologies/housing/section-variants.md)
for those spatial checks. The input sizes are not standards.

## Fix the definitions and starting allowance

Target apartment net area is 2,100 m². Here net means finished internal rooms
and private circulation, excluding all walls, shafts, shared routes/cores and
external balconies. Gross means the enclosed geometric floor plate, including
internal walls, shafts, cores and shared support; external balconies remain
outside it. Neither is asserted to be a statutory measurement category.

The preliminary budget allocates 3,260 m² residential gross G_h and
340 m² separately counted nonresidential gross G_x: G=3,600 m².
The assumed residential yield is k_h=2,100/3,260=0.6442.
The G_h definition includes residential shared access and support, including
on the ground floor; it excludes the explicitly allocated tenancies.

Three disjoint section reservations R1, R2 and E each have a 240 m² enclosed
plate repeated over five levels, giving 720 m² footprint and 3,600 m² floors.
E is a projecting **end** section, not a demonstrated turning/corner variant.

| Section | Typical dwelling count | Typical net area | Gross plate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R1 | 4 | 4*45 = 180 m² | 240 m² |
| R2 | 4 | 4*45 = 180 m² | 240 m² |
| E | 3 | 34+34+65 = 133 m² | 240 m² |

These net allowances still need actual wall, furniture, frontage, whole-door
and core verification. No access is established by placing a door point.

## Count the actual floor variants

At ground level R1 and R2 each retain one 45 m² apartment and assign 120 m²
gross to a separate tenancy. E has no ground apartments and assigns 100 m²
gross to a tenancy. Their 340 m² belongs to G_x; the rest of the ground floor,
including retained residential access, belongs to G_h under the stated study
allocation. Tenancy plans and independent routes remain to be fitted.

Levels 2–4 use the typical counts. On level 5, E retains the 34 and 65 m²
apartments; its other reservation becomes common/support space inside the
unchanged plate. R1 and R2 retain their typical level-5 apartments.

| Floor variant | Dwellings | Apartment net | Gross floors |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Ground: R1=1, R2=1, E=0 | 2 | 90 m² | 720 m² |
| Levels 2–4: 3*(4+4+3) | 33 | 3*493 = 1,479 m² | 2,160 m² |
| Level 5: 4+4+2 | 10 | 459 m² | 720 m² |
| Total | **45** | **2,028 m²** | **3,600 m²** |

The comparable residential yield is 2,028/(3,600−340)=**0.6221**, below the
assumed 0.6442. The different whole-building ratio 2,028/3,600=**0.5633**
cannot replace k_h in the residential calculation. The target has a **72 m²
shortfall**. Gross-minus-net is 1,572 m², including G_x; it is neither all
circulation nor another allowance to add to the total.

Repeating the typical floor five times instead gives 55 homes and
5*493=2,465 m², overcounting by **10 homes and 437 m²**. Preserve the actual
ground/top entries, then compare an authorized change to module mix, allocation
or height. Recheck the affected geometry and recount; relabelling the achieved
area as the target or substituting a new global factor does not resolve it.

## Evidence and use

The numerical allocation is adapted from [recovered local drafts](../../sources/index.md#recovered-local-methods)
with clarified enclosed/net boundaries and end-section identity. It introduces
no new book-reading, apartment-capacity, product or legal claim. Actual section
changes return to the existing spatial owner; the count alone establishes only
the arithmetic under these reservations.
