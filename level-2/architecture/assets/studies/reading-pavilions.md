# Two Russian reading pavilions

Start from the brief, [actual demand](../../references/program/demand.md) and accepted decisions. Choose and compare architectural consequences rather than inferring a style or regulation from a name; select [requirements](../../references/requirements.md) where applicability matters.

The following are hypothetical design studies. Their dimensions are selected to
make the geometric reasoning inspectable; they are **not legal minima, standard
room sizes, engineering sizes or performance predictions**. The area boundary is
geometric plan area; usable and legal areas would need separately defined
measurement. No project should inherit these numbers as defaults.

## Two Russian reading pavilions: climate and use change the edge

**Study facts and limits.** These are two hypothetical **new**, low-rise public
reading pavilions, one assigned to **Yakutsk**, the other to **Krasnodar**, in the
Russian Federation. Both are used and heated throughout the year, with ordinary
reading-room humidity, no catering, housing, parking or industrial process in
the study brief. Hold their room demand and area definition constant. No parcel,
survey, protected status or approval baseline is supplied. The locations are
chosen solely for a contrasting design study, not inferred from the task's
language. The primary descriptive climate observations and their historical
averaging period are identified in
[Russian climate-input selection](../../references/normative/ru/applicability.md#select-climate-site-and-assembly-inputs).
They support a contrast in seasonal conditions, not present-day design
temperatures, loads, humidity, wind, snow, rainfall intensity or calculated
comfort. Those parameters remain unverified for both studies.

**Before.** A transferable sketch puts a detached reading room and small-room
wing around an open court, with an outdoor-only link, flush unsheltered doors,
equal glazing on all fronts and a concealed gutter between high and low roofs.
It offers neither an enclosed connection nor a shade/cleaning strategy. Do not
retain it for both locations merely by changing insulation or facade color.

| Decision | Yakutsk study: cold-season internal connection is a brief priority | Krasnodar study: warm-season shaded outdoor reading is a brief priority | Reproducible check before retaining the response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parti and arrival | Join the two room groups with a common enclosed route and place a protected entrance/waiting zone inside the occupied footprint. Keep the court as optional outdoor use rather than the only connection. | Keep a direct enclosed room-to-room route but compare an additional shaded porch facing the intended outdoor reading area. Place the waiting zone under its weather protection, outside the through-route. | Trace entrance → waiting → each room with the outdoor space closed. Recount enclosed route/lobby area and lost room frontage in both; neither may borrow area invisibly from the program. |
| Openings and expression | Group view openings at occupied reading positions; compare continuous opaque field and fewer exposed opening junctions with the original undifferentiated glazing. Retain a distinct entry rather than bury it in a deep recess. | Group openings by occupied hours and actual aspect; compare a porch or controllable exterior shade with a revised opening pattern. Do not assume a horizontal overhang controls low-angle sun. | Draw the same reading position, view and sky path in plan/section. Show shade depth, support, cleaning/removal and door swing as reservations; solar/glare and thermal results remain pending. |
| Roof and water | Simplify the high/low roof valley where its only purpose was visual separation; locate accessible outlets and a deliberate overflow away from the entry. If seasonal clearing is proposed, identify its route and holding area. | Relate porch runoff and main-roof discharge to the outdoor reading edge; do not let the shaded seating zone become a drain path. Keep outlets accessible from the service approach. | Trace water from each roof area to a proposed discharge and a blocked-outlet overflow. Confirm drainage basis and seasonal operation separately; no pitch, snow-retention device or capacity is selected by city name. |
| Assembly and maintenance | Trace insulation/air continuity at the added lobby, roof junction and frame support. Keep seals/outlets inspectable and replacement access outside occupied reading positions. | Trace shade attachments through the weather boundary and test wetting/drying with the actual indoor cooling/humidity mode before deciding vapor-control placement. Keep shade controls and sill drains reachable. | Use the same head/sill/base/roof checks in [envelope and assemblies](../../references/construction/envelope/assemblies.md#adapt-the-family-to-climate-use-and-works). Compare the repair sequence and affected occupied area, not a generic material durability ranking. |
| Structure and services space | Align supports at the joined roof/route; reserve ventilation/heating distribution and replacement access without filling the public entry or blocking a clerestory. | Reserve the porch's independent support, shade controls and possible cooling/ventilation interfaces; keep intakes/discharge and maintenance clear of outdoor seating. | Overlay support, plant/shaft/distribution and drainage reservations on the same conceptual section. If they consume the desired clear space, revise roof height/location or room arrangement; specialist design determines sizes and capacities. |

**Selection and limits.** The provisional results are a compact connected
arrival/room arrangement in the first study and an enclosed route with an
additional shaded occupied edge in the second. These are architectural
responses to the stated evidence and priorities; either can change with an
actual parcel or operational brief. The descriptive climate contrast alone
does not prove compactness, glazing reduction, shade or any assembly sufficient.
If either brief instead requires an intermittently heated annex or a
moisture-producing use, revisit the
[use-dependent assembly selection](../../references/construction/envelope/assemblies.md#adapt-the-family-to-climate-use-and-works).

No parcel is available to match either proposal to a PZZ zone, GPZU, heritage
object/zone or other local establishing act; those constraints are **unresolved**,
not absent. Do not import Moscow, St Petersburg or another city's regulations
into either study. Screen the actual work/use interfaces through the Russian
module; the stated reading-only use supplies no reason to select a catering,
housing or parking document for that absent function. Reopen those branches if
an embedded or adjacent use appears. Record the selected provisions and
remaining checks with the project rather than turn these studies into city-wide
design rules or normative calculations.
