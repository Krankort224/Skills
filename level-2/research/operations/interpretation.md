# Interpretation and the next iteration

## Recover the claim being tested

Compare identified runs with their actual definition, model, prediction, and criteria; do not apply later configurations or rewritten criteria.

Assess `evidence_quality` before assigning `claim_status` (root definitions). Explain limited conclusions and missing observations.

## Determine the state of the question

Record `execution_status`, `evidence_quality`, and `claim_status` independently, linking claim IDs to check/run IDs and specific evidence.

Bound each claim to tested conditions, not the entire theory or every regime. For a universal-claim counterexample, verify assumptions and state scope.

## Separate observation from explanation

Cite specific data, table, plot, log, or proof. Separate effect from proposed mechanism; list material remaining alternatives.

For statistics, distinguish effect size, uncertainty, and practical significance; for numerical models, state tested ranges and computational-parameter dependence; for runtime checks, identify tested version/environment.

Do not present agreement with one metric, absence of discovered analogues, or an agent's expert-like assessment as proof of a mechanism or novelty. Do not assign a numerical confidence probability without a justified model for obtaining it.

## Choose the next action

Propose the next step based on the remaining unknown:

- close the local question when the answer is sufficient;
- obtain missing data or reproduce a doubtful result;
- test a rival explanation or a regime boundary;
- refine the model or hypothesis with an explicit reason for the change;
- move to a more rigorous model or physical check;
- stop/defer the direction if the nearest check is not justified by resources.

Explain the uncertainty resolved. A negative result need not change direction.

## Preserve the conclusion within its scope

Distinguish an experimental result, interpretation, project decision, and next configuration. A conclusion from one check does not automatically become a new global model or an accepted engineering decision.

When assigned a knowledge update, incorporate the durable conclusion and applicability limits at the project-defined owner; retain decision provenance and revision links.

Evaluate and report the result with material limitations and next step or stopping grounds. Project workflow owns task acceptance.
