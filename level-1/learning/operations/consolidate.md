# Decide and consolidate the outcome

Use the fixed candidate and [verification evidence](verify.md), including known failures and unavailable checks. Match findings to the governing task criteria and authority. Do not replace workflow acceptance, assigned arbitration or delegation with a learning decision.

## Choose the supported next action

| Decision | Basis | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct | A demonstrated defect has an identifiable remedy within scope and authority | Make the bounded correction and recheck the failure plus affected dependencies. |
| Rollback | The candidate is unsuitable or must be withdrawn, and its removal is authorized | Remove only its changes, preserving unrelated work and evidence; verify the resulting state. |
| Accept | Required evidence supports readiness and the governing task's acceptance authority is satisfied | Integrate/consolidate at existing owners under that process; do not infer authority from tests, review or publication. |
| Defer | A material claim/decision lacks necessary evidence, capability or authority after bounded diagnosis | Name the gap, consequence, next discriminating action and what permits resumption; continue separable authorized work. |

No-change and proposal-only results from earlier operations can finish without editing anything. A ready candidate awaiting expressly reserved acceptance remains unaccepted; route it to that existing authority. Conversely, an already authorized ordinary correction/save does not need a new Issue or extra approval ceremony.

Separate decisions by claim when necessary. A known failing behavior needs correction/withdrawal even if an independent runtime claim is deferred. Do not redefine success, erase a negative result or accept a known regression to end the cycle. Take material scope/permission decisions through the existing task owner or assigned arbiter.

## Correct and recheck

Identify the defect, bounded remedy, protected behavior and evidence needed to close it. Use the current destination and preserve changes since the reviewed revision. After correction, identify the new exact revision/artifact; rerun the failing check, original case and checks invalidated by the correction, including affected regressions/transfer boundaries. Retain unaffected evidence with its source/conditions rather than claiming all old checks cover the new version.

If the correction changes the hypothesis, scope or verification needs, record the reason before dependent work. A line changed is not a closed finding. Keep correction history and unresolved findings on the existing task surface; use assigned arbitration for adjudication and progress reassessment rather than adding a separate cycle counter or model policy here.

## Roll back only the candidate

Compare the candidate's baseline/diff with the actual current destination. Identify its specific edits, files and dependent route/test updates. Account separately for unrelated changes, supported local adaptations and later accepted work, including edits in the same file.

Apply the smallest verified inverse edit within authority. A whole-file restore, directory replacement or branch reset can erase unrelated work; use it only when ownership and comparison establish that it removes no protected changes. Check context/content before applying an inverse patch. If an overlapping edit makes the inverse ambiguous, retain the file and report the conflict for the existing owner to resolve; do not guess or force an overwrite.

Re-read the result and inspect its diff. Verify candidate additions/changes are withdrawn as intended, unrelated content is preserved, and restored routes/behavior remain coherent through affected checks. Record the withdrawn candidate, resulting revision/artifact, actual preservation checks and remaining original gap. Rollback can restore a known deficient baseline; it does not by itself resolve that gap or complete the task. Keep useful failure evidence at the task owner, not as a new universal rule.

## Accept or defer through existing owners

For an accepted improvement, place the reusable method, examples/tests and necessary provenance/limits at the canonical owner established through placement/context. Keep project facts and adaptations at their project owners. Update only authorized navigation/adoption references; publication of shared knowledge does not update downstream pins or installations automatically.

Use the current task's established result/acceptance record. For an Issue, workflow owns the accepted summary, body reconciliation and comment cleanup; learning does not duplicate them in an archive or close the Issue. Where arbitration is assigned, return the evidence and unresolved decisions to the arbiter.

For deferral, state what is supported now, what is not established, the missing source/runtime/permission and why it matters, and a concrete resumption condition. Preserve the scoped candidate/proposal and known failures without promoting them to accepted knowledge. Missing evidence does not become favorable through silence or elapsed time; a safely repairable defect within authority should be corrected rather than indefinitely deferred.
