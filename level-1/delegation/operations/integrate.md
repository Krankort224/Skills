# Verify and integrate contributions

Use when contributions arrive or before final delivery. Apply the [shared contract](../SKILL.md). A child report is evidence to assess, not automatic acceptance of its claims.

## Check direct-child outputs

Match each result to its assignment and source/artifact revision:

- requested coverage and deliverable, including omissions and out-of-scope changes;
- compliance with material role, permission, write and required-skill requirements;
- source pointers, actual checks and what those checks establish;
- unresolved questions, failed checks, missing evidence and dependent consequences;
- runtime completion state, including relevant descendant/background execution.

Inspect supporting evidence proportionally. A skill-name acknowledgement, success status or process exit does not prove the requested property. Do not automatically repeat all research when the supplied evidence is sufficient; resolve concrete remaining risks or required gates.

Separate documented claims, agent observations and native-runtime checks. A simulated assignment can show how an agent applied the instructions; it cannot certify another host's side effects. Keep requested/effective model or configuration differences visible when they affect the result.

## Resolve disagreements and changed inputs

Compare conflicting conclusions at the appropriate source owner and scope. Seek the smallest discriminating evidence rather than deciding by model tier, confidence, majority or recency alone. Keep material uncertainty explicit. Route requirement/authority disputes to the assigned owner or arbiter where applicable.

Return corrections to the assigned writer with evidence and a closure condition. A reviewer does not acquire write authority over the reviewed area by finding a small defect. Use [recovery](recover.md) for an unsuitable executor or changed decomposition.

When an artifact or material input changes, fix the new revision and determine which checks and dependent contributions it invalidates. Recheck affected properties and interfaces; preserve still-valid evidence explicitly. Do not apply an earlier review to a new combined result without that assessment.

## Check the combined result

Trace each completion requirement to a contribution and adequate check. Verify that individually plausible outputs agree at their boundaries: shared definitions, decisions, source pins, interfaces and current artifact state. Inspect the combined changes for gaps, duplicated/conflicting work and unrelated changes.

Include required documentation/navigation contributions and their established author/checker boundaries. Keep canonical-source decisions with context and the assigned process; delegation adds no blanket documentation rewrite or independent-review obligation.

If a task requires a non-author check, identify the actual checker and evidence; self-review is insufficient. If it does not, do not add an unnecessary second participant merely to satisfy a role pattern. A missing mandatory check remains a stated readiness gap.

## Finish execution and hand off

Account for every launched participant and relevant descendant/background task, including queued retries or automatic resumptions: complete and collect, cancel with confirmation where authorized, or report the specific unresolved execution. Do not declare overall completion while unaccounted work can still modify the result. A legitimate ongoing-service handoff must already be within the task's scope and identify its owner/state.

Return the verified combined result, exact revision/artifacts, material checks, outstanding limitations and next action. Checkpoint enough active/pending state for a blocked continuation without copying canonical sources or full transcripts.

Use workflow's handoff/status/acceptance procedure only when the task actually concerns an Issue. Execution completion, publication, user acceptance and Issue closure remain distinct. Preserve prior explicit authorization within its scope; neither successful checks nor silence supplies additional authority.
