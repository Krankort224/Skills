# Collect, recover and check contributions

Follow work until the parent can use the contribution or name the remaining gap. Use the existing [assignment and return contract](dispatch.md#prepare-an-accessible-task-package). Keep partial findings, evidence, artifacts and role boundaries through interruption and correction.

## Follow work and establish its state

Keep the assignment, input and result revisions, owner, relevant child/task identifiers, dependencies and pending work recoverable on the existing task surface when needed. On context loss reconstruct these details from task records and live evidence before acting. Prefer notifications or suitably spaced waits. Continue disjoint work, and use a checkpoint or interim report when expectations, risk or repeated non-progress warrant it.

| State | Meaning to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Receipt | The tool or child acknowledges a request. Read the actual launch outcome to establish whether work started. |
| Attempt | The recorded run of an assignment, with an associated identity/state when available. An interruption or timeout may leave useful evidence and unresolved effects. |
| Result | A returned report or artifact at a particular version. Inspect its sufficiency and provenance. |
| Retry or continuation | A deliberate new or resumed attempt with current inputs and a supported remedy, or a host-queued resumption. Track its relation to the earlier attempt. |
| Settlement | The assigned writer and any relevant background or queued execution have finished or been stopped with observable confirmation. A tool receipt, attempt end or missing report cannot establish settlement by itself. |

## Resolve incomplete work

Check the latest material progress, available partial artifacts, current permissions and actual dependencies. Diagnose the cause before retrying or changing executors.

| Supported cause | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Missing or misunderstood input | Provide the discriminating source or requirement. |
| Contradictory requirement or reserved decision | Return the specific decision to its owner. Suspend affected work only. |
| Missing access, tool or permission | Use an already authorized capable route or report the exact limitation. |
| Coupled work or pending dependency | Reunite coupled choices, await the necessary input, or explicitly reshape dependencies. |
| Execution or verification error | Give the assigned writer the specific correction and an observable closure condition. |
| Inadequate model on a well-defined available task | Reassess the allowed choices using [model selection](../references/model-selection.md). |
| Stall or unclear state | Seek an interim result and determine whether progress, a changed condition or settlement is possible. |

Preserve useful work. Reuse the child when its context, route and permissions still fit. Give a continuation or replacement the [complete assignment package](dispatch.md#prepare-an-accessible-task-package), updated inputs, existing findings and checks, the diagnosed cause, changed conditions and the check for recovery. Keep approved rights and required independent checks. A stronger model cannot supply missing access or authority. Do not repeat a failed attempt under unchanged conditions. The parent may complete a part it can perform in scope with the required checks.

Before transferring a write area, confirm the previous writer and relevant processes, descendants and queued or auto-resumable attempts are settled. A paused or limit-stopped writer may still resume. If authorized, cancel obsolete work and verify the resulting state. Without settlement evidence, keep overlapping writes blocked and continue disjoint work. Evaluate late results against their original assignment and pins before reuse. Preserve open findings and the cause of cancellation.

## Check contributions and the combined result

1. Match each report and artifact to its assignment and exact input/result versions. Inspect expected coverage, permitted effects, actual changes, required methods, findings, source pointers and check results. An acknowledgement or process exit gives limited evidence. Distinguish source inspection, an agent's observed decision and an actual runtime effect.
2. Compare conflicting findings at the source that can resolve them. Preserve the material findings and seek the smallest discriminating check. Keep unresolved requirements with their owners. A reviewer sends a correction and closure criterion to the assigned writer. If the result or input changes, fix the new version, recheck affected work and dependencies, and identify which earlier evidence still applies.
3. Fix and inspect the combined revision. Trace each requirement to its contribution and check. Verify interfaces, consistent source pins, necessary documentation, unresolved findings and the absence of unrelated effects. Confirm each mandatory check was performed by the required checker on the right version. Self-review cannot fill a non-author check.
4. Account for every launched child and relevant descendant, queued retry and background process. Return the usable combined result, its exact artifacts and revision, checks, evidence limits and remaining gaps. A scoped ongoing handoff needs its existing authority, owner and state. Completion here grants no publication, merge, acceptance or closure.

Stop when the evidence supports the assignment's result or the remaining dependency is explicit.
